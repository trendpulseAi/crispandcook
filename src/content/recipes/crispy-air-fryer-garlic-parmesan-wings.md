# Crispy Air Fryer Garlic Parmesan Wings

These crispy air fryer garlic Parmesan wings have golden skin, juicy meat, and a buttery garlic-Parmesan coating. They’re easy to make in about 30 minutes and delicious as a quick dinner, party appetizer, or game-day snack.

## Why You Will Love This Recipe

The air fryer circulates hot air around the wings, crisping their skin without a deep fryer or a pot of oil. A little baking powder helps the skin brown and turn extra crunchy, while tossing the wings in garlic-Parmesan butter after cooking keeps the cheese and garlic flavorful—not scorched.

## Ingredients

For the wings:

- **2 lb chicken wings (900 g)**, split into flats and drumettes, tips removed
- **1 tbsp aluminum-free baking powder (8 g)** — not baking soda
- **¾ tsp fine salt (4 g)**
- **½ tsp black pepper (1 g)**
- **1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)**

For the garlic-Parmesan coating:

- **3 tbsp unsalted butter (42 g)**
- **3 garlic cloves (about 9 g)**, finely minced
- **½ cup finely grated Parmesan (50 g)**
- **1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (4 g)**
- **1 tsp lemon juice (5 ml)**, optional

## Equipment Needed

- Air fryer
- Large mixing bowl
- Paper towels
- Small saucepan or microwave-safe bowl
- Instant-read thermometer

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Dry the wings.** Pat the chicken wings thoroughly dry with paper towels. Removing surface moisture helps the skin crisp.

2. **Season.** In a large bowl, mix the baking powder, salt, black pepper, and garlic powder. Add the wings and toss until evenly coated.

3. **Preheat the air fryer.** Set it to **400°F (200°C)** and preheat for 3 minutes, if your model requires preheating.

4. **Arrange the wings.** Place the wings in the air fryer basket in a single layer, leaving a little space between pieces. Cook in batches if needed; overcrowding can make the wings steam instead of crisp.

5. **Air-fry and turn.** Cook at **400°F (200°C) for 10 minutes**. Turn each wing, then cook for **another 10 minutes**. Check for crisp, browned skin and an internal temperature of at least **165°F (74°C)** at the thickest part, away from the bone. If the wings need more browning, air-fry for 2–4 additional minutes, checking often.

6. **Make the garlic-Parmesan butter.** While the wings cook, melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat or in the microwave. Stir in the minced garlic and cook gently for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant. Remove from the heat and stir in the Parmesan, parsley, and optional lemon juice.

7. **Toss and serve.** Transfer the hot wings to a clean bowl. Pour the garlic-Parmesan mixture over them and toss to coat. Serve immediately for the crispiest texture.

## Chef's Secrets for Maximum Crispiness

- **Dry the wings well.** A thorough pat-down is one of the simplest ways to encourage crisp skin.
- **Use baking powder, not baking soda.** Baking powder helps the skin brown; baking soda can leave an unpleasant taste.
- **Keep the basket uncrowded.** Arrange the wings in one layer with space around them, and cook in batches if necessary.
- **Turn halfway through.** Flipping at the 10-minute mark helps the wings brown evenly.
- **Add the Parmesan coating after cooking.** Garlic and cheese can burn in the air fryer, so toss them with the wings once they’re crisp.
- **Check the temperature.** Chicken should reach at least **165°F (74°C)**. Wings often become especially tender at a higher temperature, so cook a little longer if you prefer.

## Variations & Substitutions

- **Add heat:** Toss in a pinch of cayenne pepper or red pepper flakes.
- **Make them lemony:** Add extra lemon juice or a little lemon zest to the finished coating.
- **Use dried herbs:** Replace the fresh parsley with 1 teaspoon dried parsley or Italian seasoning.
- **Try a different cheese:** Pecorino Romano makes a saltier, sharper coating. Reduce the added salt slightly if using it.
- **Use frozen wings:** Thaw them completely and pat them very dry before seasoning for the best crispness.

## Storage, Reheating & Freezing Instructions

- **Refrigerate:** Cool leftover wings, then store them in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3–4 days.
- **Reheat:** Air-fry at **375°F (190°C) for 5–8 minutes**, turning once, until hot and crisp. Add a little fresh Parmesan after reheating if desired.
- **Freeze:** Freeze cooked, cooled wings in an airtight freezer-safe container for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator, then reheat in the air fryer at **375°F (190°C)** until hot and crisp.
