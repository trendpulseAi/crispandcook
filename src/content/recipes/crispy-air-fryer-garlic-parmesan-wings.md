These Crispy Air Fryer Garlic Parmesan Wings are crunchy on the outside, juicy inside, and coated in a savory mix of butter, fresh garlic, and Parmesan. They’re easy to make for game day, a casual get-together, or whenever you’re craving wings without ordering takeout.

The air fryer is a great fit for wings because its circulating heat crisps the skin without deep-frying or leaving you with a pot of oil to deal with. The key is to dry the wings well and give them room in the basket; cook in batches if necessary so the hot air can reach every side.

The garlic butter and Parmesan come together while the wings cook, so you can toss everything together and serve while it’s hot. For the best texture, grate the Parmesan fresh and add it just before serving.

## Crispy Air Fryer Garlic Parmesan Wings

**Prep time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook time:** 20 minutes  
**Serves:** 4

### Ingredients

- 2 pounds fresh chicken wings, split into flats and drumettes
- 1 tablespoon aluminum-free baking powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley

### Instructions

1. **Preheat the air fryer** to 380°F (193°C) if your model recommends preheating. Pat the wings very dry with paper towels.
2. **Season the wings.** In a large bowl, toss them with the baking powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
3. **Air fry.** Arrange the wings in a single layer in the basket, leaving space around each piece. Cook at 380°F (193°C) for 13 minutes. Flip the wings, raise the temperature to 400°F (204°C), and cook for 7 minutes more, until golden and crisp. Work in batches rather than crowding the basket.
4. **Make the garlic butter.** Melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat or in a microwave-safe bowl. Stir in the minced garlic and let it sit for about a minute.
5. **Toss and serve.** Transfer the hot wings to a clean bowl and toss with the garlic butter. Add the Parmesan and parsley, toss again, and serve immediately.

Check the thickest part of a wing with an instant-read thermometer, avoiding the bone. Chicken should reach at least 165°F (74°C); wings are often especially tender when cooked to about 175–185°F (79–85°C).

### Tips for the crispiest wings

- Dry the wings thoroughly before seasoning. If you have extra time, leave them uncovered on a rack in the refrigerator for a few hours to dry the skin further.
- Leave space between the wings in the basket. Crowding traps steam and can make the skin soft.
- Add the Parmesan just before serving so it coats the hot wings without getting soggy.
- If cooking in batches, hold finished wings on a wire rack in a low oven while the next batch cooks.

### Storage and reheating

Refrigerate leftover wings in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat them in the air fryer at 360°F (182°C) for 4–5 minutes, checking that they’re heated through and crisp again.
