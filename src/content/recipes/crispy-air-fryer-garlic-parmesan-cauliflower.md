This crispy air fryer garlic Parmesan cauliflower brings together golden edges, a savory coating, and tender centers in an easy side dish for everyday dinners. The ingredients are simple and budget-friendly, and most of the hands-on work is just cutting and tossing.

The air fryer’s circulating heat helps brown the florets all around without a hot pan to tend or the longer wait for a full oven to heat. Give the pieces space in the basket, and they can turn crisp at the edges while staying pleasantly tender inside. A quick shake partway through helps them cook evenly.

### Ingredients

- 1 medium cauliflower, cut into bite-sized florets
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
- 1/8 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/3 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/3 cup finely grated Parmesan cheese, divided

### Instructions

1. Pat the cauliflower dry and cut it into florets about 1 to 1 1/2 inches across.
2. In a large bowl, toss the florets with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Add the panko and half the Parmesan. Toss again to coat; a few loose crumbs are fine.
4. Preheat the air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** if your model recommends it. Arrange the cauliflower in a single layer, leaving a little space between pieces. Cook in batches if necessary.
5. Air fry for **10 minutes**, then shake the basket or turn the florets. Sprinkle on the remaining Parmesan and cook for **3–5 minutes** more.
6. Check for deeply golden edges, a crisp coating, and florets that are tender when pierced with a fork. Serve hot.

### Serving and storage

Serve this cauliflower as a side with chicken, fish, or a simple bowl of rice and beans. Refrigerate leftovers in a covered container for **3–4 days**, cooling and storing them within 2 hours of cooking. Reheat in a single layer at **375°F (190°C)** for about **3–6 minutes**, checking until hot and crisp. Freezing is safe, though the texture will be softer after thawing; freeze in a single layer, transfer to a freezer-safe container, and use within about 2 months for best quality.

### FAQs

**Can I make this ahead of time?**  
You can wash and cut the cauliflower up to a day ahead. Refrigerate it in a sealed container and pat it dry before seasoning. For the crispest result, cook shortly before serving.

**How do I avoid sogginess?**  
Dry the florets thoroughly and leave space between them in the basket. If they look crowded, cook in batches rather than piling them up.

**Can I double the recipe?**  
Yes. Cook in batches unless your air fryer can fit the larger amount in a roomy single layer. Keep the first batch warm on a rack in a low oven while the rest cooks, then serve promptly.
