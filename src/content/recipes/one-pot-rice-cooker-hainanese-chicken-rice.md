Hainanese chicken rice delivers poached, tender chicken over rich rice infused with aromatics. Doing the entire dish in a standard rice cooker saves pans and keeps the meat moist without constant watching.

The essential trick is searing the aromatics in oil before cooking or mixing them evenly with the rinsed grains. This coats each grain of rice so it cooks evenly without clumping or turning into mush.
