Plump shrimp and tender broccoli florets cook together in one air fryer basket for a fast, balanced weeknight dinner in ten minutes. The shrimp get remarkably crunchy while the broccoli develops crisp, roasted edges.

The real trick is a light cornstarch dusting rather than a wet batter, which traps moisture inside the shrimp without turning soggy. Toss everything with an easy sweet chili mayo sauce right before serving so the crunch stays intact.
