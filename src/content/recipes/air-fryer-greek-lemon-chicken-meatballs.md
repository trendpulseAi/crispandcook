Making poultry-based meatballs on the stovetop often feels like an uphill battle. Between delicate ground chicken sticking to the pan, hot oil spattering across the counters, and meatballs flattening out into awkward patties, the traditional skillet approach can be frustrating. That is why the air fryer has completely transformed how our test kitchen approaches ground meat. 

The intense, rapid-circulation heat of an air fryer envelopes each meatball simultaneously, setting the outer protein structure before the balls have a chance to slump. You get uniform, beautifully browned spheres with a delightfully crisp exterior while the interior stays tender and juicy. Because the basket is perforated, any excess moisture or rendered fat drains directly into the bottom pan, meaning your meatballs sear cleanly rather than stewing in their own liquids.

These Greek-inspired lemon-herb chicken meatballs are a masterclass in budget-friendly weeknight cooking. Ground chicken provides an economical canvas for pungent garlic, earthy oregano, bright lemon zest, and briny pockets of feta cheese that melt just enough without leaking away. Pair them with warm pita bread, cucumbers, and a scoop of tzatziki for a high-flavor dinner that goes from prep bowl to table in under twenty-five minutes.
