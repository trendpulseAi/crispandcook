steps:
  - title: "Rinse and Drain the Grains"
    text: "Place the jasmine rice in a fine-mesh sieve and rinse under cold running water for 45 to 60 seconds, agitating with your fingers until the water runs mostly clear. Tap the sieve against the sink edge to remove excess water thoroughly."
  - title: "Season the Protein"
    text: "Toss the diced chicken thigh pieces with the kosher salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, and ground cumin in a medium bowl until every piece is evenly coated in the dry spice blend."
  - title: "Layer the Rice Cooker Pot"
    text: "Transfer the well-drained rice directly into the inner bowl of the rice cooker. Pour in the low-sodium chicken broth, add the minced garlic, and gently stir to distribute. Scatter the diced chorizo evenly across the top of the grains. Layer the seasoned chicken thigh pieces over the chorizo in a single, even stratum without stirring them down into the rice."
  - title: "Cook on Standard Cycle"
    text: "Close the rice cooker lid securely. Select the standard 'White Rice' or 'Regular Cook' cycle (which automatically heats between 212°F and 220°F / 100°C and 105°C before dropping to warm). Let the machine run completely uninterrupted until the cycle switches automatically to 'Keep Warm' (approx. 30 to 35 minutes). The chicken will be opaque, tender, and register at least 165°F (74°C) internally."
  - title: "Rest and Fold In Greens"
    text: "Quickly open the lid, scatter the frozen sweet peas across the hot surface, and snap the lid back shut immediately. Allow the cooker to rest on the 'Keep Warm' setting undisturbed for 10 minutes; the trapped steam will gently heat the peas through without turning them dull or mushy."
  - title: "Fluff and Serve"
    text: "Open the cooker and use a nonstick rice paddle to gently fold the chicken, chorizo, and peas into the spiced rice, lifting from the bottom of the pot to distribute all rendered oils evenly. Spoon into warm bowls and finish with a scattering of freshly chopped parsley."

When weeknights get chaotic, the humble rice cooker becomes the ultimate culinary shortcut. While most home cooks relegate this countertop appliance exclusively to plain steamed grains, its sealed heating chamber actually behaves like an automated, ultra-efficient steam oven. By utilizing the cooker's gentle, steady thermal regulation, you can simultaneously steam proteins to tender perfection while allowing their seasoned pan drippings to flavor the starch simmering right underneath.

This one-pot smoky chicken and chorizo rice takes full advantage of that thermodynamic magic. As the unit heats, the diced cured chorizo begins sweating out its natural, vibrant paprika-infused oils. Instead of being lost to a pan, that aromatic fat drips directly into the jasmine rice below, ensuring every forkful is deeply savory, aromatic, and perfectly separated rather than dry or bland.

Best of all, this recipe requires less than ten minutes of actual prep, eliminates the danger of scorching rice on the stovetop, and leaves you with just a single nonstick pot to rinse at the end of the night. It is practical, deeply satisfying, and designed to squeeze maximum flavor out of budget-friendly pantry staples.
