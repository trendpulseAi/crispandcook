This pilaf turns a handful of affordable staples into a bright, filling dinner: green salsa and chiles flavor the rice while corn adds sweetness, and black beans make each bowl more substantial. A little lime at the end keeps the flavors lively, while pepitas bring welcome crunch.

The Rice Cooker does the hands-off steaming for you, and cooked shredded chicken warms on top while the rice cooks below. It’s a useful way to make a complete meal with minimal cleanup—especially when you have leftover rotisserie chicken to use up.
