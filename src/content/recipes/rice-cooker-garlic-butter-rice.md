steps:
- title: Rinse the rice
  text: Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh sieve under cool water until it runs less cloudy, then drain well. Check that your cooker permits the addition of garlic and butter before proceeding.
- title: Add to the cooker
  text: Put the rice, water, minced garlic, butter, and salt in the inner pot. Stir briefly. Use the liquid quantity recommended by your rice cooker if it differs, and never exceed its maximum fill line.
- title: Cook
  text: Close the lid and select the white-rice setting. A cycle commonly takes around 20 to 35 minutes, but the timing varies by cooker. Follow your appliance instructions and its minimum and maximum capacity.
- title: Rest and fluff
  text: Once the cycle ends, leave the rice covered for about 5 minutes if your cooker instructions allow it. Open carefully, fluff with a rice paddle, and check that the grains are tender.
- title: Serve
  text: Taste and adjust the seasoning, then sprinkle with optional parsley. Serve hot as a side with your choice of vegetables or protein.
proTips:
- Use the same measuring cup for the rice and water to keep the proportions consistent.
- Cut the garlic finely for a more even distribution, or slice it thinly if you prefer noticeable pieces.
- If the rice is too firm or soft, consult your cooker instructions and adjust the water slightly on a future batch.
- For extra color, garnish with a few garlic slices browned separately in a skillet, if desired.
storage: 'For leftovers, spread the rice into a clean, shallow container and cool it quickly, ideally within 1 hour. Do not leave cooked rice cooling in the rice cooker. Cover and refrigerate promptly at 40°F (4°C) or below, and use within 24 hours.


  Reheat only once, until the rice is steaming hot throughout and reaches 165°F (74°C). Add a small splash of water if it seems dry, and stir so it heats evenly. Reheating does not make improperly stored rice safe, so discard rice that has been left out too long.


  For longer storage, divide freshly cooked rice into shallow portions, cool promptly, and freeze in airtight containers. Thaw in the refrigerator and reheat once until hot throughout.'

Garlic butter rice is a simple side dish with a savory flavor and a comforting, fluffy texture. This rice-cooker version combines jasmine rice with garlic and a little butter, using familiar ingredients that work with a wide range of meals.

Cooking times and liquid requirements vary between rice cookers, so use the recipe as a starting point and follow your appliance’s instructions. Rinsing the rice and measuring the liquid consistently can help you get a texture you enjoy.

Serve the rice with vegetables, chicken, fish, or beans. A little parsley adds color, and optional garlic browned separately can provide a deeper flavor.
