steps:
- title: Drain and press the tofu
  text: Drain the tofu and wrap it in a clean kitchen towel or paper towels. Place a light weight on top for about 10 minutes to remove some moisture. Pat dry and cut into roughly 1-inch (2.5 cm) cubes.
- title: Add the seasoning
  text: Place the cubes in a bowl and gently toss with the oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Sprinkle over the cornstarch and toss again until lightly coated.
- title: Arrange in the air fryer
  text: Preheat the air fryer to 375°F (190°C) if your appliance recommends it. Place the tofu in a single layer with space between the cubes. Cook in batches if your basket is small.
- title: Air fry until golden
  text: Cook for approximately 12 to 16 minutes, shaking the basket or turning the cubes halfway through. Check early, as cooking times vary. The tofu is ready when the coating is golden and the pieces are hot throughout.
- title: Serve
  text: Transfer the tofu to a serving bowl and sprinkle with green onion if desired. Serve with rice and vegetables, or add it to a salad.
proTips:
- Dry the tofu well before seasoning; extra surface moisture can make the coating less crisp.
- Cut the cubes to similar sizes and leave space in the basket so they cook evenly.
- Add a favorite sauce after cooking to keep the coating from softening too quickly.
- For a simple dinner, serve the tofu with cooked rice and steamed vegetables.
storage: 'Refrigerate leftover tofu in a shallow, covered container within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep it at 40°F (4°C) or below and use it within 3 to 4 days.


  To reheat, spread the tofu in the air fryer or oven until the center reaches 165°F (74°C). The coating will generally be softer than it was when first cooked, but reheating in a single layer can help improve its texture.


  Freezing cooked tofu can change its texture, so refrigeration is a good option when you plan to use leftovers soon. If you freeze it, use a freezer-safe container, thaw in the refrigerator, and reheat thoroughly.'
faqs:
- q: Can I use soft tofu?
  a: Extra-firm tofu is best for this recipe because it holds its shape when pressed and tossed. Soft or silken tofu can break apart.
- q: Why is my tofu not crisp?
  a: Too much moisture or an overcrowded basket can affect the texture. Pat the tofu dry, use a light coating, and arrange the cubes in a single layer.
- q: Can I make the tofu without cornstarch?
  a: Yes. Leave it out and cook the seasoned cubes as directed, though the exterior will have a different, less crisp texture.

Air fryer tofu is an easy way to make a simple ingredient feel more satisfying. These garlic-seasoned cubes have a lightly crisp outside and a tender center, making them a useful addition to rice bowls, salads, and quick dinners.

The main step is removing some moisture before seasoning. A short press and a light dusting of cornstarch help the cubes brown, while leaving space in the basket lets hot air reach their edges.

Serve the tofu with cooked rice and vegetables, or pair it with a sauce you enjoy. Add any sauce after cooking if you want to preserve more of the crisp coating.
