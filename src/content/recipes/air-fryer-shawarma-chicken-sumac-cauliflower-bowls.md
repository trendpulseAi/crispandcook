steps:

These shawarma-inspired bowls bring plenty of bold flavor to an everyday pack of chicken thighs. Cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, and a little cinnamon make a fragrant coating, while sumac gives the cauliflower a lively lemony tang.

The Air Fryer is especially handy here because it browns the chicken and cauliflower with just a little oil—and each cooks quickly in its own batch. Add cool yogurt sauce and something crisp, like cucumber or lettuce, for an easy dinner that also works tucked into warm pita.
