These air fryer salmon bites are a simple option when you want a quick meal with minimal preparation. Garlic, paprika, and Italian seasoning add a savory flavor, while a squeeze of lemon gives the finished dish a fresh touch.

Cutting the salmon into evenly sized pieces helps it cook consistently. Serve the bites with rice, vegetables, or a simple salad.
