- amount: 1 US cup (about 170 g)
  item: uncooked white quinoa
  why: White quinoa produces a mild-tasting side dish.
- amount: 1 3/4 cups (420 ml)
  item: water
  why: This is a starting amount; follow the quinoa package and your cooker instructions if they differ.

  item: salt, or to taste

substitutions:
- swap: white quinoa
  with: Use another color of quinoa and follow its package instructions; cooking time and texture can differ.
- swap: water
  with: Use a broth only if your rice cooker manufacturer permits it; reduce the added salt as needed.

  with: Leave them out or add another fresh herb to taste.
steps:
- title: Check your rice cooker
  text: Check the manufacturer’s instructions for quinoa, the recommended liquid ratio, setting, and minimum and maximum amounts. Do not exceed the maximum fill line.
- title: Rinse the quinoa
  text: Place the quinoa in a fine-mesh sieve and rinse thoroughly under cool running water. Drain well before cooking.
- title: Add to the inner pot
  text: Put the rinsed quinoa, water, and salt in the rice cooker. Stir briefly. Use the liquid amount recommended for quinoa by your appliance or package if it differs from the amount listed here.
- title: Cook
  text: Close the lid and use the quinoa setting if available, or the setting recommended in your manual. A cycle may take around 20 to 35 minutes or longer, depending on the machine.
- title: Rest, fluff, and serve
  text: When the cycle finishes, let the quinoa rest covered for about 5 minutes if your manual allows it. Fluff gently with a fork or suitable rice paddle and check that the grains are tender. Serve hot with optional lemon and parsley.
proTips:
- Use a fine-mesh sieve so the small grains do not fall through when rinsing.
- Measure the quinoa and liquid consistently; the cup supplied with a rice cooker may not equal a US cup.
- If the grains are still firm or excess water remains, follow your manufacturer’s directions for finishing the cycle.
- Serve as a side with vegetables, chicken, fish, or beans.
storage: 'Cool leftover quinoa promptly in a clean, shallow container, then cover and refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 3 to 4 days.


  Reheat leftovers until they reach 165°F (74°C) throughout. A splash of water can help loosen the grains; stir during reheating for more even heating.


  For longer storage, freeze cooled portions in freezer-safe containers. Thaw in the refrigerator and reheat thoroughly before serving. Add fresh parsley and lemon after reheating.'
faqs:
- q: Do I need to rinse quinoa?
  a: Rinsing in a fine-mesh sieve can remove some of the natural coating that tastes bitter. Check the package, since some quinoa is pre-rinsed.
- q: Can every rice cooker cook quinoa?
  a: Not necessarily. Check your appliance manual for suitable grains, recommended settings, and capacity before cooking quinoa.
- q: Why is my quinoa too soft or too firm?
  a: Liquid amounts and cooker settings vary. Use the directions for your appliance and quinoa, and adjust the amount of water on a future batch if needed.

Quinoa makes a simple, versatile side dish that pairs with vegetables, beans, chicken, or fish. This rice-cooker version finishes the tender grains with optional lemon and parsley for a fresh flavor.

Rinsing the quinoa and measuring the liquid consistently are useful starting points. Rice cookers vary, so check that your appliance supports quinoa and follow its recommended setting and water ratio.

Serve it warm alongside a main dish, or cool and refrigerate leftovers promptly for another meal.
