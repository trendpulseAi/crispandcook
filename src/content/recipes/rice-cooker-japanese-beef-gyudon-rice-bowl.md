Gyudon is Japanese comfort food at its most practical: tender beef and slivered onions simmered in dashi, soy sauce, and mirin over rice. Making it in a rice cooker turns it into a genuine one-pot meal without dirtying a skillet.

The trick is layering. Keep the seasoned meat and raw onions on top of the rinsed grains and broth so the rice steams evenly below while the beef fat renders into the cooking liquid.
