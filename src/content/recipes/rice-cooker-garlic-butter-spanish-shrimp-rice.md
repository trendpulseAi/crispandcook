This one-pot meal combines the vibrant flavors of Spanish garlic shrimp with seasoned tomato and paprika rice, streamlined for everyday cooking. Using the rice cooker eliminates multiple pans while allowing every grain of rice to absorb savory broth, sweet garlic, and rich butter.

Adding raw, seasoned shrimp near the end of the cooking cycle gently steams them to plump perfection without becoming rubbery. It is a reliable, hands-off dinner that delivers restaurant-quality flavor with practically zero cleanup.
