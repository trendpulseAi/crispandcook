Crispy salt and pepper tofu is a takeout favorite that translates exceptionally well to the home kitchen using an air fryer. By dusting well-pressed, cubed extra-firm tofu in a light coating of cornstarch and seasonings, the concentrated convection heat creates an ultra-crisp exterior without the mess or heavy oil of deep frying.

Once crisped, the golden bites are tossed with aromatic scallions, minced garlic, and thinly sliced chili peppers for a punchy, savory lunch. Pair them with a bowl of warm jasmine rice or serve over crisp salad greens for a balanced, protein-packed midday meal.
