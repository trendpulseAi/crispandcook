steps:
- title: Dry thoroughly
  text: Spread the rinsed chickpeas on a clean kitchen towel. Pat dry carefully, then leave uncovered for about 5 minutes while you measure the seasoning. Remove any loose skins if desired; peeling every chickpea is unnecessary.
- title: Season lightly
  text: Toss with the oil and salt. Reserve the paprika and garlic powder until near the end so they are less likely to scorch.
- title: Arrange the basket
  text: Preheat to 390°F (200°C) if your manual recommends it. Add chickpeas in one layer. Cook in batches if necessary, and do not use loose parchment that could lift toward the heating element.
- title: Air fry and shake
  text: Cook for 12 to 18 minutes, shaking every 4 to 5 minutes. At about 12 minutes, toss with the paprika and garlic powder in a heatproof bowl, then return to the basket. Check frequently during the final minutes; the chickpeas should be golden with dry, crisp surfaces.
- title: Cool briefly
  text: Let stand for about 5 minutes before tasting; the centers are very hot. They firm up a little as they cool. Adjust salt and serve plain or with optional parsley just before eating.
proTips:
- Moist chickpeas need longer and may remain chewy; careful drying matters more than extra oil.
- If spices darken before the chickpeas crisp, reduce the heat and check more often.
- Add fresh herbs only to the portion you are serving immediately; their moisture softens stored chickpeas.
storage: 'These chickpeas are crispest shortly after cooking. Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 3 to 4 days. This is a moist home-cooked snack, not a shelf-stable packaged product, so do not assume it can sit in a jar on the counter for several days.


  To reheat, spread the chickpeas in the air fryer basket and cook at 350°F (175°C) for about 3 to 5 minutes, shaking once and checking early. Heat leftovers to 165°F (74°C), then let them stand briefly before eating. Avoid adding more oil unless the surface seems especially dry. Freezing is possible for about 1 month for quality, but the texture will soften. faqs:
- q: Why are my chickpeas chewy?
  a: They may have been too wet or crowded. Dry thoroughly, cook in a single layer, and allow a short cooling period before judging the texture.
- q: Can I use dried chickpeas straight from the bag?
  a: No. Dried chickpeas must be soaked and fully cooked first. This recipe starts with cooked chickpeas.
- q: Will they stay crunchy overnight?
  a: Not usually as crunchy as when freshly made. Refrigerate leftovers for safety and briefly re-crisp before serving.

A can of chickpeas becomes a smoky, savory snack with very little hands-on work. These air fryer chickpeas also add crunch to a bowl of soup or a simple salad.

The important step happens before cooking: drain and dry the chickpeas well. Add the powdered seasonings toward the end, when the surfaces have started to crisp, to keep the garlic and paprika from tasting burnt.

Cooking time depends on the size of the chickpeas and your appliance, so start checking early rather than relying on the timer alone.
