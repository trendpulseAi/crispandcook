A rice cooker makes these green chile chicken bowls especially easy because the rice steams right in a savory mix of broth, salsa verde, and spices. While it cooks, the beans and corn warm on top, so you get a filling one-pot base without juggling a saucepan.

Using cooked rotisserie chicken keeps the recipe weeknight-friendly and helps the meat stay tender: stir it in after the rice is done, then let the cooker warm everything through with cheddar. A squeeze of lime and a handful of crunchy tortilla strips turn simple pantry staples into a dinner that feels like a treat.

This is a flexible, low-fuss meal for busy evenings. Keep the salsa mild for family-friendly bowls, or choose a spicier jar and add extra lime and cilantro at the table.
