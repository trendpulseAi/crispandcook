These air fryer peanut chicken tenders deliver bold, Thai-inspired flavors with minimal prep and cleanup. Marinating tender strips of chicken breast in a blend of creamy peanut butter, soy sauce, lime, and red curry paste creates a rich, savory crust while locking in moisture.

Air frying cooks the chicken quickly without drying it out, producing lightly caramelized edges and juicy meat in under fifteen minutes. Pair these flavorful tenders with steamed jasmine rice, a fresh cucumber salad, or extra peanut dipping sauce for an effortless weeknight dinner.
