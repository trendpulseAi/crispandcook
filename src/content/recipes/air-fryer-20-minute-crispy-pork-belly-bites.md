steps:
- title: Prepare the pork
  text: Pat the raw pork belly dry with paper towels. Using a clean cutting board and knife, cut into roughly 3/4-inch (2 cm) cubes. Wash hands and any surfaces that touched raw pork.
- title: Season
  text: Combine garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Toss the pork with the seasoning. No extra oil is needed because pork belly contains plenty of fat.
- title: Arrange the basket
  text: Preheat the air fryer to 380°F (190°C) if your manual recommends it. Place the pork in a single layer with space between the pieces. Cook in batches if needed and follow your appliance’s instructions for cooking fatty foods.
- title: Air fry
  text: 'Cook at 380°F (190°C) for about 18 to 22 minutes, turning the bites halfway. Begin checking early: smaller pieces or a hotter machine may cook sooner. Pork belly releases hot fat; if you notice excessive smoke, stop the machine and follow the manufacturer’s directions for safely handling accumulated grease. Never pour water into hot grease.'
- title: Check and rest
  text: Use a food thermometer inserted into the thickest part of several pieces, avoiding the basket, to check for at least 145°F (63°C). Rest for 3 minutes before eating. If you prefer browner edges, cook a little longer, watching closely. Transfer to a clean plate and serve with optional green onions or sauce.
proTips:
- The cook time is per batch and includes a 3-minute rest; allow more time if your basket requires multiple batches.
- 'Avoid crowding the basket: excess moisture makes browning harder.'
- Carefully dispose of cooled grease according to local guidance; do not pour it down the sink.
storage: 'Refrigerate leftover pork in a shallow covered container within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the surrounding temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and eat within 3 to 4 days. Store sauces separately so the pork is less likely to soften. Do not leave cooked pork on the counter for the rest of the evening.


  For reheating, arrange the bites in a single layer in the air fryer and heat at 350°F (175°C), checking after about 3 minutes. Continue as needed until the centers reach 165°F (74°C), checking several pieces with a thermometer. Timing varies with piece size and appliance. Reheat only the portion you plan to eat and handle any rendered fat carefully. For longer storage, freeze promptly cooled portions in airtight freezer-safe packaging for up to 2 months for best quality. Thaw in the refrigerator, not on the counter, and reheat thoroughly. The texture may be less crisp after freezing, but a few minutes in the air fryer can help restore the browned edges.'
faqs:
- q: Why are my pork belly bites not crispy?
  a: The pork may have been wet or the basket crowded. Pat dry, leave space between pieces, and cook a little longer if necessary, watching for excess smoke.
- q: Can I use bacon instead of pork belly?
  a: Not as a direct substitute. Bacon is typically cured and thinly sliced, so it cooks differently. This recipe uses raw, uncured, skinless pork belly.
- q: How do I know the pork is cooked safely?
  a: 'Check the thickest part of several pieces with a food thermometer: pork should reach at least 145°F (63°C), then rest for 3 minutes. Do not rely on color alone.'

These savory pork belly bites turn golden in the air fryer with just a few pantry seasonings. Serve them as an appetizer with your favorite dip or as a main dish alongside rice and vegetables.

The cooking time is roughly 20 minutes per batch, plus prep and a short rest. Cut the pork evenly and give each piece space for the best browning.
