# One-Pot Rice Cooker Garlic Butter Chicken and Rice

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
The rice cooker acts as a sealed steam chamber, which cooks the rice from the bottom while gently poaching the chicken in garlic-infused steam from above. As the butter melts, it drips through the chicken pieces straight into the rice grains along with the rendered poultry juices. The core trick is keeping the chicken resting *on top* of the leveled rice rather than mixing it in; this prevents dense pockets of meat from trapping water and creating undercooked, crunchy rice kernels.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Appliance:** Any standard 4-cup to 10-cup rice cooker. A basic single-switch model works just as well as an expensive fuzzy-logic unit.
- **Rinse the rice thoroughly:** Run cold water over the rice in a fine-mesh strainer for at least 30 seconds until the water runs mostly clear. Skipping this leaves excess surface starch in the pot, which mixes with the melted butter to create a gummy, gluey texture.
- **Dry and size the meat:** Blot the chicken dry with paper towels before cutting. Cut the meat into uniform **1-inch chunks**. If you cut them larger, the rice cycle will click off before the chicken reaches a safe internal temperature.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Boneless, skinless chicken thighs:** 1 lb (450g) – trimmed of large fat slabs and cut into 1-inch pieces (thighs stay juicy; breasts dry out fast).
- **Jasmine or long-grain white rice:** 1.5 cups (300g) – rinsed and drained.
- **Low-sodium chicken broth:** 1.75 cups (420ml) – gives far better depth than plain tap water.
- **Unsalted butter:** 2 tbsp (28g) – cut into 4 or 5 small pats.
- **Fresh garlic:** 4 cloves – finely minced (about 1 generous tablespoon).
- **Soy sauce:** 1 tbsp (15ml) – low-sodium preferred to season the chicken.
- **Kosher salt:** 1/2 tsp (3g) – adjust if using regular table salt.
- **Black pepper:** 1/4 tsp (1g) – freshly cracked.
- **Scallions (optional):** 2 stalks – thinly sliced for serving.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Season the Chicken:** Place the cut chicken into a small prep bowl. Add 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Toss with your hands until evenly coated. Set aside while assembling the pot.
2. **Layer the Base:** Pour the rinsed, drained rice into the rice cooker inner pot. Pour in the 1.75 cups of chicken broth. Use a spoon to gently spread the rice into an even, flat layer across the bottom.
3. **Add Aromatics and Butter:** Scatter the minced garlic across the surface of the rice. Drop the butter pats evenly over the top. **Do not stir.** Stirring pushes the butter and garlic to the bottom where they can scorch against the heating element.
4. **Position the Chicken:** Lay the seasoned chicken pieces on top of the rice in a single layer. Make sure the chicken sits above the grains rather than buried inside them.
5. **Cook Cycle:** Close the lid. Press the standard "Cook" or "White Rice" button. Run the standard cycle, which takes roughly **20 to 25 minutes**.
6. **The Resting Step (Crucial):** When the machine switches to "Warm," leave the lid shut and let it steam undisturbed for **5 minutes**. Opening it immediately dumps all the trapped heat needed to finish cooking the chicken.
7. **Finish Cue & Fluff:** Open the lid. Check the thickest chicken chunk with a digital thermometer; it must read at least **165°F (74°C)**. Use the rice paddle to gently fold the chicken, melted garlic butter, and fluffy rice together. Scatter sliced scallions over the top and serve.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **The rice turns out soggy or mushy:** You added too much liquid. Chicken releases natural juices as it cooks. If you use the standard 1:2 ratio of rice to water, your pot will have too much liquid. Stick to the 1.5 cups rice to 1.75 cups liquid ratio to account for the meat's moisture.
- **The chicken is pink or undercooked:** You cut the pieces too thick, or opened the lid the second the cooker clicked to "Warm." If your meat thermometer reads below **165°F (74°C)**, immediately close the lid and let it sit on the "Keep Warm" setting for another 7 to 10 minutes. The residual heat will finish the job.
- **The bottom layer of rice scorches:** You stirred the aromatics or butter into the bottom before starting. Always layer: rice on the bottom, broth next, aromatics/fats on top, chicken resting above everything.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Chicken Thighs $\rightarrow$ Chicken Breasts:** Use what is on sale. If using breast meat, cut the pieces slightly larger (about 1.5 inches) so they do not dry out during the steam cycle.
- **Chicken Broth $\rightarrow$ Water + Bouillon Cube:** Don't waste money buying liquid cartons of broth. Dissolve 1 chicken bouillon cube (or 1 tsp Better Than Bouillon) in 1.75 cups of warm water.
- **Fresh Garlic $\rightarrow$ Garlic Powder:** If you don't have fresh cloves on hand, swap with 3/4 tsp garlic powder tossed directly into the chicken marinade.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge:** Transfer leftovers into an airtight glass or plastic container within 1 hour of cooking. Store in the refrigerator for up to **4 days**.
- **Reheat in the Rice Cooker:** Place cold leftover chicken and rice back into the rice cooker pot. Add **2 tablespoons of water or chicken broth** over the top to generate steam. Close the lid and press "Cook" or "Quick Cook" for **6 to 8 minutes** until the rice is hot and the chicken reaches **165°F**. Do not reheat without adding liquid, or the bottom layer will crisp and harden before the center warms through.
