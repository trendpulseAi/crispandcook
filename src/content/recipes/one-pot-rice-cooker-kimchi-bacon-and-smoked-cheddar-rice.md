# One-Pot Rice Cooker Kimchi Bacon and Smoked Cheddar Rice

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Cooking the bacon and kimchi directly on top of the rice lets the rendered pork fat and fermented chili brine seep down into the grains as they steam. The rice cooker maintains a controlled, sealed environment that cooks the rice through without drying out the bacon. The core trick is holding back a small portion of your normal cooking water to balance out the liquid from the kimchi juice. Adding the smoked cheddar at the very end on the warm setting ensures the cheese melts into a smooth, creamy sauce instead of breaking into an oily mess.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Appliance:** Any standard 4-to-10-cup rice cooker works. A nonstick inner pot is mandatory; the melted cheese and caramelized kimchi sugars will weld themselves to unlined stainless steel.
- **Rinse the rice:** Wash your rice in cold water until the water runs mostly clear (about 3 to 4 changes of water). Excess surface starch combined with bacon fat creates a sticky, gummy paste instead of distinct, fluffy grains.
- **Do not stir before cooking:** Layer the bacon and kimchi flat over the raw rice. If you mix everything together before running the cycle, the meat and vegetables sink to the bottom, insulate the heat sensor, and trigger an undercooked batch or a burnt bottom.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Jasmine or medium-grain white rice:** 2 standard rice measuring cups (approx. 360g / 1 3/4 US cups) – rinsed and drained thoroughly.
- **Thick-cut bacon:** 5 strips (approx. 170g / 6 oz) – cut into 1/2-inch pieces. Thin bacon shrivels into rubber; thick-cut stays meaty.
- **Well-fermented kimchi:** 1 cup (180g) – chopped into bite-sized bits. Older, sour kimchi works best here.
- **Reserved kimchi juice:** 2 tbsp (30ml) – strained directly from the jar.
- **Low-sodium chicken broth:** 1 1/2 cups (360ml) – use water if you don't have broth on hand.
- **Soy sauce:** 1 tbsp (15ml) – regular dark or all-purpose soy sauce.
- **Smoked cheddar cheese:** 1 cup (100g) – freshly shredded. Pre-shredded cheese is coated in starch and won't melt smoothly.
- **Scallions:** 2 stalks – finely sliced, whites and greens separated.
- **Toasted sesame oil:** 1 tsp (5ml) – added at the finish for aroma.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prep Step:** Dump the rinsed and drained rice into the rice cooker pot. Pour in the chicken broth, reserved kimchi juice, and soy sauce. Gently shake the pot side-to-side so the rice settles in an even, flat layer under the liquid.
2. **Layering Step:** Scatter the chopped kimchi and white parts of the scallions evenly over the surface of the rice. Lay the raw diced bacon over the kimchi in a single layer. **Do not stir the pot.** The ingredients must sit directly on top of the liquid-submerged rice.
3. **Cook Step:** Secure the lid and start your rice cooker on the standard **White Rice** cycle (or press the single cook lever down). The cycle will run for **30 to 35 minutes**. Leave it completely alone; do not open the lid mid-cycle or you will dump the steam needed to cook the bacon through.
4. **Finish Cue:** When the cooker clicks over to the **Keep Warm** setting, open the lid. The bacon should look fully opaque, rendered, and cooked through, with no raw pink spots. Immediately dump in the shredded smoked cheddar, toasted sesame oil, and sliced scallion greens. 
5. **Fold and Rest:** Use the rice paddle to slice down to the bottom of the pot and fold the rice, bacon, and melting cheese together until creamy and uniform. Close the lid and let it sit on **Keep Warm for 3 to 5 minutes** so the cheese fully emulsifies into the grains. Serve hot.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **Mistake 1: The rice turned out wet and soggy.** Kimchi contains a lot of water. If you didn't subtract liquid to account for the kimchi juice, you drowned the rice. *Fix:* Leave the lid open on the **Keep Warm** setting for 8 to 10 minutes, fluffing occasionally to let the excess steam escape.
- **Mistake 2: The bottom burned and the top rice is crunchy.** You stirred the bacon into the rice before hitting start. The heavy pork settled onto the bottom heating element and triggered the thermal cutoff early. *Fix:* Add 3 tablespoons of hot water, close the lid, and hit the **Cook** cycle again for another 5 minutes to steam the top layer through.
- **Mistake 3: The cheese turned into an oily puddle.** You added the cheese while the pot was at a rolling boil or left it on high heat. High temperatures break cheese emulsions. *Fix:* Only fold the cheese in *after* the cook cycle finishes, using the residual steam to melt it gently.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Bacon:** Swap with 1/2 can of diced Spam or cheap pork hot dogs. Brown them slightly in a pan first if you want extra flavor, or toss them in raw just like the bacon.
- **Smoked Cheddar:** Don't waste money buying specialty cheese if you don't have it. Use standard sharp cheddar, mozzarella, or Monterey Jack, and add 1/4 teaspoon of smoked paprika directly to the cooking liquid.
- **Chicken Broth:** Use plain tap water. The bacon fat, kimchi brine, and soy sauce provide more than enough sodium and glutamate to carry the dish.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge:** Store leftovers in an airtight glass container for up to **4 days**. The fat from the bacon and cheese will firm up solid in the cold.
- **Reheat in the Rice Cooker:** Put the cold rice back into the nonstick rice cooker bowl and sprinkle **1 to 2 tablespoons of water** over the top. Close the lid and hit the **Quick Cook** or regular **Cook** cycle for **8 to 10 minutes**. Once the pot gets hot and starts steaming, cancel the cycle, stir the rice to redistribute the melted fat, and serve. Never microwave this on high power without a cover, or the bacon fat will splatter and dry the rice out completely.
