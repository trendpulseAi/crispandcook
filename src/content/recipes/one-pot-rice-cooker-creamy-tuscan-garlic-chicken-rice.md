Tuscan chicken turns standard white rice into a rich, savory skillet-style dinner straight from your rice cooker. Tender chicken thighs steam over long-grain white rice soaked in chicken broth, garlic, and sun-dried tomato oil.

The trick to keeping the dairy from curdling is simple: cook the rice and meat in broth first, then fold in heavy cream, parmesan, and fresh spinach on the warm cycle right before serving.
