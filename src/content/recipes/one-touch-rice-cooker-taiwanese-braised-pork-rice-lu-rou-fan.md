Lu Rou Fan is iconic Taiwanese comfort food: finely diced pork belly braised in soy sauce, warm five-spice, and fried shallots until glossy and fork-tender.

The trick to pulling this off on a weeknight is using a standard rice cooker. The tightly sealed lid traps braising steam, rendering rich pork fat gently without scorching the sugars or demanding constant stovetop babysitting.
