storage: |
  To store leftovers, let the chicken cool to room temperature (no longer than 2 hours), then place it in an airtight container. It keeps in the fridge for up to 3–4 days. To keep the skin from going soggy, lay a paper towel in the container to absorb moisture.

  To freeze, wrap each cooled thigh individually in plastic wrap or foil, then place them in a freezer bag with the air pressed out. Label with the date and freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.

  The best way to reheat is in the air fryer: place the thighs skin-side up at 370°F (188°C) for 4–6 minutes, until hot all the way through (165°F / 74°C). This brings the skin back to crispy instead of rubbery like the microwave does. If the chicken looks dry, brush on a few drops of oil before reheating. You can also shred cold leftovers for wraps, rice bowls, or salads.
