- amount: 1 US cup (about 185 g)

- swap: canned coconut milk
  with: Use water for plain rice if your appliance does not permit coconut milk; the finished dish will not have the same coconut flavor.
- swap: green onion
  with: Leave it out or use a little chopped fresh cilantro if you enjoy its flavor.
steps:
- title: Check your rice cooker
  text: Check that your model permits coconut milk and that this quantity meets its minimum and maximum fill instructions. Rice cookers vary; follow the manufacturer’s rice-to-liquid guidance if it differs from this recipe.
- title: Rinse the rice
  text: Place the rice in a fine-mesh sieve and rinse under cool water until the water runs less cloudy. Drain well before adding it to the cooker.
- title: Add the liquid and seasoning
  text: Add the rice, stirred coconut milk, water, and salt to the inner pot. Stir to combine. Never exceed the cooker’s maximum fill line.
- title: Cook the rice
  text: Close the lid and select the white-rice setting. A cycle may take around 20 to 35 minutes depending on the appliance. Follow its instructions and stop the cooker if it foams excessively or the manufacturer advises against the ingredients.
- title: Rest and serve
  text: When the cooking cycle finishes, let the rice rest covered for about 5 minutes if your cooker instructions allow it. Fluff gently with a rice paddle, check that the grains are tender, and serve hot with optional green onion.
proTips:
- Measure the rice and liquid with the same US measuring cup; the small cup supplied with many rice cookers is a different size.
- Stir the coconut milk before measuring so the separated cream is evenly mixed.
- Follow your cooker’s minimum and maximum capacity; a very small cooker may not be suitable for this recipe.
- Serve with grilled vegetables, fish, chicken, or a simple bean dish.
storage: 'For leftovers, spread the rice into a clean, shallow container and cool it quickly, ideally within 1 hour. Do not leave cooked rice cooling in the rice cooker. Cover and refrigerate promptly at 40°F (4°C) or below, and use within 24 hours.


  Reheat only once, until the rice is steaming hot throughout and reaches 165°F (74°C). Add a small splash of water if it seems dry, and stir so it heats evenly. Reheating does not make improperly stored rice safe, so discard rice that has been left out too long.


  For longer storage, divide freshly cooked rice into shallow portions, cool promptly, and freeze in airtight containers. Thaw in the refrigerator and reheat once until hot throughout.'

- q: Can I cook coconut milk in any rice cooker?

- q: Why is my coconut rice too soft or too firm?

- q: How should I store leftover coconut rice?
  a: Cool the rice quickly, ideally within 1 hour, refrigerate promptly, and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once until steaming hot throughout.

Coconut rice is a comforting side dish with a gentle coconut flavor and a soft, fluffy texture. This rice-cooker version uses jasmine rice, unsweetened coconut milk, and a little salt for a simple accompaniment to everyday meals.

Rice cookers differ in their recommended liquid amounts and the ingredients they allow, so check your appliance instructions before you begin. Measuring the rice and liquids consistently will also help you adjust the recipe to your cooker.

Serve the rice with grilled vegetables, chicken, fish, or beans. A few slices of green onion add a fresh finish if you like.
