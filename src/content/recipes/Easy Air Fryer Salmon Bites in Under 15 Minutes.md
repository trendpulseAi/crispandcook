If you are looking for a quick, healthy, and incredibly flavorful dinner after a busy day, these **Air Fryer Salmon Bites** are about to become your new favorite recipe.

By cooking bite-sized salmon pieces in the air fryer, you get a beautiful golden crust on the outside while keeping the inside tender, juicy, and flaky—all in less than 15 minutes!

### Why You’ll Love This Recipe
* **Super Fast:** Takes less than 15 minutes from prep to plate.
* **Minimal Ingredients:** Uses simple pantry spices you already have.
* **Crispy & Juicy:** The air fryer circulates hot air to give the salmon bites an unbeatable crispy texture.

### Ingredients Needed
* **Salmon Filets:** 1 lb (about 450g) skinless salmon, cut into 1-inch bite-sized cubes.
* **Olive Oil:** 1 tablespoon.
* **Garlic Powder:** 1 teaspoon.
* **Paprika:** 1 teaspoon.
* **Italian Seasoning:** 1/2 teaspoon.
* **Salt & Black Pepper:** 1/2 teaspoon each.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Prep the Salmon
Pat the salmon dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture. Cut the salmon into uniform 1-inch cubes.

#### Step 2: Season the Bites
Place the salmon cubes into a bowl. Add olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper. Gently toss.

#### Step 3: Air Fry to Perfection
Preheat your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)**. Arrange the salmon bites in a single layer in the air fryer basket.

#### Step 4: Cook
Air fry at **400°F (200°C) for 7 to 9 minutes**, gently shaking the basket halfway through.

#### Step 5: Serve
Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the top and serve hot!
