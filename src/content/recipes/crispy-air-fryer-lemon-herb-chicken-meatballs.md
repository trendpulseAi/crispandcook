# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Ground chicken dries out fast when baked in an oven and turns into an oily mess when pan-fried. The air fryer solves both problems: rapid convection heat browns the exterior crust in just **12 minutes** while keeping the interior juicy. Lemon zest adds bright acidity without dumping excess liquid into the meat mixture, and a small hit of salty feta provides internal pockets of fat that keep lean poultry tender. 

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Basket Size:** A standard 4-quart or larger air fryer fits a full batch (16 meatballs) in one layer. If you have a compact 2-to-3-quart basket, cook in two batches. If they touch, they steam instead of crisping.
- **Ditch the aerosol spray:** Never spray propellant-based nonstick sprays (like Pam) directly onto your air fryer basket; the propellants degrade the nonstick coating. Use an oil mister or brush vegetable or olive oil directly onto the grate.
- **Wet your hands:** Ground chicken is notoriously wet and sticky. Keep a small bowl of cold water nearby and wet your palms before shaping each ball so the meat doesn't glue to your skin.

## Ingredients & Measurements

- **Ground chicken:** 1 lb (450g) – Use 92/8 or 85/15 lean ground chicken. Avoid 99% fat-free ground chicken breast or your meatballs will taste like cardboard.
- **Panko breadcrumbs:** 1/2 cup (25g) – Japanese panko keeps them lighter than dense Italian breadcrumbs.
- **Egg:** 1 large – Binds the mixture so the meatballs don't fall apart during the mid-cook shake.
- **Feta cheese:** 1/3 cup (50g), crumbled – Buy a block in brine and crumble it yourself; pre-crumbled feta is coated in anti-caking starch that prevents it from melting properly.
- **Fresh lemon zest:** 1 tbsp (from 1 medium lemon) – Save the juice for squeezing over the cooked meatballs at the table.
- **Garlic:** 3 cloves, finely minced or grated.
- **Dried oregano:** 1 tsp – Dried works better than fresh here; it infuses into the fat without burning.
- **Fresh parsley:** 2 tbsp, finely chopped – Stems removed.
- **Kosher salt:** 3/4 tsp (use 1/2 tsp if your feta is exceptionally salty).
- **Black pepper:** 1/2 tsp, freshly ground.
- **Olive oil:** 1 tsp (for brushing the basket or a quick spray over the tops).

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Preheat & Mix:** Preheat your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** for **3 minutes**. In a large bowl, combine the ground chicken, panko, beaten egg, crumbled feta, lemon zest, minced garlic, oregano, parsley, salt, and black pepper. Mix with your hands or a fork just until combined. Stop mixing as soon as everything is incorporated—overworking ground poultry turns it dense and rubbery.
2. **Portion the Meatballs:** Wet your hands with cold water. Divide the mixture into 16 evenly sized portions (about 1.25 inches in diameter, roughly 1 to 1.2 oz / 30g each). Roll them gently between your palms into rounds.
3. **Load the Air Fryer:** Lightly brush the preheated air fryer basket with olive oil. Arrange the meatballs in a single layer, leaving at least a 1/2-inch gap between each one. Lightly mist the tops with olive oil to kickstart the browning.
4. **Air Fry:** Cook at **400°F (200°C)** for **10 to 12 minutes**. At the **6-minute mark**, pull the basket out and give it a firm shake or use silicone-tipped tongs to turn each meatball. They should be firm enough to flip without tearing.
5. **Check Doneness:** Pull the basket at **11-12 minutes**. Check the center of the largest meatball with an instant-read meat thermometer; it must register **165°F (74°C)**. The outsides should be deeply browned and speckled with crisp, golden bits of feta. Transfer to a plate immediately so the residual heat from the basket doesn't dry them out.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)

- **Mistake 1: Meatballs turn out dry and tough.** You likely used 99% lean ground chicken breast or let them cook past 165°F. Fix: Buy standard ground chicken (which includes dark meat). If you can only find lean breast meat, add 1 tablespoon of olive oil directly into the raw meat mixture.
- **Mistake 2: The meatballs stick and tear when flipping.** You tried to turn them too early or didn't grease the basket grate. Fix: Let them cook undisturbed until the 6-minute mark. The heat will naturally sear and release the meat from the metal.
- **Mistake 3: Pale, soggy crust.** The air fryer basket was overcrowded. Fix: If the meatballs are touching, moisture gets trapped and steams the meat. Run two separate batches of 8 if your basket is small.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions

- **The Meat:** Ground turkey (93/7) works as an exact 1:1 substitute without changing cook times. Ground pork also works and yields an even juicier meatball.
- **The Binder:** Don't waste money buying panko if you don't have it. Use standard dry breadcrumbs (reduce amount to 1/3 cup / 35g) or crush up saltine crackers. Quick oats run through a blender for five seconds also work cleanly.
- **The Feta:** If feta is out of your budget, swap in an equal amount of shredded block mozzarella or sharp white cheddar. If skipping cheese entirely, add 1 tablespoon of olive oil to the meat mixture to compensate for the lost fat.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions

- **Fridge:** Store leftover air fryer greek lemon chicken meatballs in a sealed, airtight glass container for up to **4 days**. 
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer:** Do not microwave these—the microwave turns ground chicken into rubber and makes the crust soggy. Pop them back into the air fryer at **350°F (175°C)** for **3 to 4 minutes** until heated through and crisp on the outside.
- **Freezer:** Freeze cooked meatballs on a baking sheet for 1 hour until solid, then transfer to a freezer-safe zip-top bag for up to **3 months**. Reheat directly from frozen in the air fryer at **360°F (182°C)** for **8 to 9 minutes**.
