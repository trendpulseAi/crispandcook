# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Ground chicken dries out into rubber if it sits in a slow oven for 25 minutes. The air fryer solves this by circulating concentrated **400°F (200°C)** convection heat around the meat, searing a brown crust on the outside in under 12 minutes before the center can lose its moisture. The core trick here is grating the onion directly into the meat mixture rather than dicing it. The released onion juice acts as an internal moisture barrier that keeps lean chicken juicy without needing cups of heavy filler.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Basket Space**: A standard 4-quart to 6-quart air fryer basket holds 12 to 14 meatballs comfortably. If your basket is smaller, cook in two batches—crowding causes steaming instead of browning.
- **Preheat Required**: Always preheat your air fryer for **3 minutes at 400°F (200°C)**. Dropping raw chicken onto a cold basket grid causes the meat to stick and tear when flipped.
- **Wet Your Hands**: Ground chicken is wetter and stickier than ground beef. Keep a small bowl of cold water nearby and dip your fingers before rolling each ball so the meat doesn't cling to your skin.

## Ingredients & Measurements

- **Ground chicken (90/10 or 92/8 blend)**: 1 lb (450g) – *Avoid 99% lean ground breast meat; it lacks the fat needed to brown and dry-heaves in the air fryer.*
- **Panko breadcrumbs**: 1/2 cup (25g) – *Gives a lighter texture than dense regular breadcrumbs.*
- **Egg**: 1 large – *Beaten lightly to bind.*
- **Yellow onion**: 1/4 cup (50g) – *Grated on a box grater, juice included.*
- **Garlic**: 3 cloves – *Minced fine or run through a press.*
- **Fresh lemon zest**: 1 tbsp – *Zest from 1 medium lemon. Do not skip; juice alone won't provide concentrated flavor.*
- **Lemon juice**: 1 tbsp (15ml) – *Squeezed fresh from the zested lemon.*
- **Dried oregano**: 1 tsp (1.5g) – *Rub between your palms to crush before adding.*
- **Fresh parsley**: 2 tbsp (8g) – *Finely chopped.*
- **Feta cheese (optional)**: 1/4 cup (35g) – *Crumbled small. Leave out if keeping ingredient costs down.*
- **Kosher salt**: 1 tsp (5g)
- **Black pepper**: 1/2 tsp (1.5g)
- **Olive oil spray**: For coating the basket and meat surfaces.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Build the Binder**: In a large mixing bowl, combine 1/2 cup panko, 1 beaten egg, 1/4 cup grated onion with its juices, 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp lemon zest, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp dried oregano, 2 tbsp parsley, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp black pepper. Stir until it forms a wet paste (panade) and let sit for **2 minutes** so the breadcrumbs soften.
2. **Combine Gently**: Add the 1 lb ground chicken (and crumbled feta, if using). Use clean hands or a fork to fold the mixture together just until uniform. Stop the moment there are no dry pockets left—overworking turns chicken paste-like and rubbery.
3. **Portion and Roll**: Wet your palms with water. Portion the mixture into 12 equal-sized balls (roughly 1.5 inches in diameter, or 35-40g each). Place them on a clean plate or cutting board.
4. **Preheat the Air Fryer**: Set your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** and run it empty for **3 minutes**.
5. **Load the Basket**: Lightly mist the hot air fryer basket with olive oil spray. Place the meatballs into the basket in a single layer, leaving at least 1/2-inch of space around every meatball. Mist the tops lightly with olive oil spray to jump-start browning.
6. **Cook and Flip**: Cook at **400°F (200°C)** for **6 minutes**. Open the basket, gently roll/flip each meatball over with tongs (or give the basket a firm, quick shake).
7. **Finish and Check**: Cook for another **4 to 6 minutes** (10 to 12 minutes total). The exterior should be deeply golden and slightly blistered. Insert an instant-read meat thermometer into the center of the largest meatball; it must read at least **165°F (74°C)**. Pull them immediately to prevent overcooking.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)

- **Meatballs are rubbery and dry**: This happens if you used 99% lean white breast meat or overworked the mixture. Next time, buy a 90/10 dark/white meat grind, and stir only until combined. If you're stuck using extra-lean meat, mix 1 tbsp of olive oil straight into the raw meat mix.
- **Meatballs stick and tear on the flip**: The basket wasn't hot enough or lacked oil when the raw meat hit the grate. Always preheat for **3 minutes** and give the basket a fast mist of oil right before adding the chicken.
- **Pale, soggy surfaces without browning**: The basket is overcrowded, trapping steam. If all 12 meatballs can't sit with breathing room between them, split the batch into two runs of 6 meatballs each.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions

- **Breadcrumbs**: Don't buy a brand-new tub of panko if you have saltine crackers or plain bread. Crush 6 saltine crackers finely or blitz a stale slice of sandwich bread; both absorb moisture just as well.
- **Fresh Herbs**: If fresh parsley is limp or overpriced at the store, substitute with 1 additional teaspoon of dried oregano or dried Italian seasoning.
- **Meat Choice**: This recipe works dollar-for-dollar with ground turkey (85/15 or 93/7) or ground pork. If using pork, skip the extra oil spray on top since pork renders sufficient fat on its own.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions

- **Fridge**: Let cool completely, then store in an airtight glass or plastic container for up to **4 days**. 
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Never microwave these—the crust turns spongy and the chicken dries out. Preheat the air fryer to **350°F (175°C)**. Toss the cold meatballs into the basket and heat for **3 to 4 minutes** until the exterior is sizzling and crisp again.
