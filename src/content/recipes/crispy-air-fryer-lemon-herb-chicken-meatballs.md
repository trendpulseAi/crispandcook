# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Standard ovens take 20 minutes to cook ground chicken, which evaporates surface moisture and leaves you with dry, rubbery meat. The air fryer circulates high-velocity heat around the meatballs, searing the outer crust in just **12 minutes** while locking juices inside. Lemon zest—not juice—provides intense Greek citrus flavor without adding excess water that ruins the browning process.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Appliance Check**: A standard 4-quart to 6-quart basket-style air fryer works best. If your basket is under 4 quarts, cook in two batches. Crowding makes the meatballs steam into pale, soggy blobs instead of crisping.
- **Wet Your Hands**: Ground chicken is far stickier than ground beef. Keep a small bowl of cold water nearby and dip your fingers before shaping each meatball to stop the meat from clinging to your palms.
- **Zest Before Juicing**: Zest the lemon directly into the bowl before slicing it open. Once you cut a lemon in half, zesting it cleanly without scraping your knuckles is nearly impossible.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Ground chicken (92/8 lean)**: 1 lb (450g) – Avoid 99% fat-free ground chicken breast; it turns into sawdust.
- **Panko breadcrumbs**: ½ cup (30g) – Absorbs fat and keeps the interior light.
- **Feta cheese**: ⅓ cup (50g), crumbled – Adds salty moisture pockets.
- **Egg**: 1 large – Binds the mixture so meatballs don't crumble through the basket grate.
- **Lemon zest**: 1 tbsp (from 1 medium lemon) – Pure citrus oil flavor without excess liquid.
- **Garlic**: 3 cloves, finely minced – Use fresh, not the jarred paste which burns bitter.
- **Dried oregano**: 1 tsp – Greek oregano preferred for punchier aroma.
- **Fresh parsley**: 2 tbsp (about 8g), finely chopped – Stems removed.
- **Kosher salt**: ½ tsp – Go light; feta already brings salt.
- **Black pepper**: ¼ tsp, freshly cracked.
- **Olive oil cooking spray**: For coating the basket and meatballs.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Build the Mix**: In a large mixing bowl, lightly beat the egg with a fork. Add the ground chicken, panko, crumbled feta, lemon zest, minced garlic, dried oregano, chopped parsley, salt, and black pepper. Use clean hands or a fork to fold the ingredients together just until combined. Stop as soon as the panko disappears—overmixing results in dense, rubbery hockey pucks.
2. **Portion and Roll**: Wet your hands with cold water. Scoop out rounded portions (about 2 tablespoons each, roughly the size of a golf ball) and roll them between your palms into 12 to 14 uniform balls. 
3. **Preheat and Prep the Basket**: Preheat your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** for **3 minutes**. Spray the air fryer basket lightly with olive oil spray.
4. **Load the Air Fryer**: Arrange the meatballs in the basket in a single layer with at least a half-inch of space between each piece. Give the tops of the meatballs a light mist of olive oil spray to kickstart browning.
5. **Cook**: Air fry at **400°F (200°C)** for **10 to 12 minutes**. 
6. **The Mid-Point Shake**: At the **7-minute mark**, pull the basket out and give it a firm shake, or use silicone-tipped tongs to rotate each meatball. This exposes the unbrowned bottoms to the direct heat flow.
7. **Check Doneness**: The meatballs are done when the exteriors are deeply golden-brown with sizzling edges, and an instant-read meat thermometer inserted into the center of the largest meatball reads **165°F (74°C)**. Pull them immediately to keep them from drying out.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **The meatballs stick to the basket wires**: This happens if the air fryer wasn't preheated or you didn't oil the grate. Don't try to rip them off early. Wait until the crust forms at minute 6; they will release naturally from the metal.
- **The meatballs are dry and tough**: You bought 99% lean ground chicken breast or left them in past 165°F. Next time, buy 92/8 blend or mix 1 tablespoon of olive oil directly into ultra-lean meat before shaping.
- **The meatballs flatten out into discs**: The mix was too wet from using lemon juice or oversized eggs. Add 2 to 3 tablespoons of extra panko to firm up the mix, and chill the raw meatballs in the fridge for **15 minutes** before cooking.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Panko Breadcrumbs**: Use crushed saltine crackers, dry bread crusts blitzed in a food processor, or quick oats pulsed twice in a blender.
- **Feta Cheese**: Swap with grated parmesan or finely grated sharp white cheddar. Don't waste money buying an entire tub of feta if you already have a block of hard cheese in the drawer.
- **Ground Chicken**: 93/7 lean ground turkey works with zero changes to cook time or temperature. Ground pork also works, though you will want to wipe out excess rendered fat midway through.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge**: Store cooked meatballs in a sealed, airtight glass or plastic container for up to **4 days**. Let them cool completely before snapping the lid on so trapped steam doesn't make the crust soggy.
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Skip the microwave—it turns ground poultry rubbery. Set your air fryer to **375°F (190°C)** and reheat the meatballs for **3 to 4 minutes** until hot throughout and crisp on the outside.
