# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Ground chicken dries out fast in a conventional oven, but an air fryer's tight convection chamber blasts the exterior with direct heat, browning the surface before the center loses its juices. The trick here is using lemon zest instead of lemon juice in the meat mixture—juice adds excess acid and water that breaks down the proteins into mush, while zest infuses concentrated citrus oils without altering the binder. Pockets of salty feta melt against the hot air currents, giving these **air fryer greek lemon chicken meatballs** a caramelized, crispy edge in just **12 minutes**.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Basket Size**: Tested in a 5.8-quart square basket. If you're using a smaller 3-to-4-quart unit, cook in two batches. Overcrowding drops the chamber temperature and steams the meatballs instead of crisping them.
- **Wet Your Hands**: Ground chicken is notoriously wet and sticky. Keep a small bowl of cold water next to your prep station and dip your fingers into it before rolling each meatball so the meat doesn't cling to your skin.
- **Ditch Aerosol Sprays**: Non-stick air fryer baskets peel over time if you use canned cooking sprays containing soy lecithin or propellants. Use a refillable oil mister filled with regular olive oil or avocado oil.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Ground Chicken (92/8 blend)**: 1 lb (450g) – Avoid 99% lean ground chicken breast; it turns into dry chalk. Ground turkey (93/7) works just as well.
- **Panko Breadcrumbs**: 1/2 cup (30g) – Regular breadcrumbs work, but Japanese panko creates lighter, crispier meatballs.
- **Feta Cheese**: 1/3 cup (50g), crumbled – Buy block feta in brine if you can; pre-crumbled tubs are coated in cellulose and don't melt as cleanly.
- **Egg**: 1 large – Lightly beaten to bind the meat mixture.
- **Lemon Zest**: 1 tbsp (from about 1 medium lemon) – Grate only the yellow skin. Stop when you hit the bitter white pith.
- **Garlic**: 2 cloves, finely minced – Grating on a microplane spreads the garlic flavor evenly without leaving raw chunks.
- **Dried Oregano**: 1 tbsp – Crush it between your palms before adding to release the dried oils.
- **Fresh Parsley**: 2 tbsp, finely chopped – Stems removed.
- **Kosher Salt**: 1/2 tsp – If using fine table salt, drop this to 1/4 tsp to prevent over-salting alongside the feta.
- **Black Pepper**: 1/4 tsp – Freshly cracked.
- **Olive Oil Spray**: Propellant-free, for coating the basket and meatballs.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Mix the Base**: In a large mixing bowl, lightly whisk the 1 large egg, minced garlic, lemon zest, dried oregano, chopped parsley, salt, and black pepper. Stir in the 1/2 cup panko and let it sit for **1 minute** to absorb the liquid.
2. **Combine and Portion**: Add the 1 lb ground chicken and 1/3 cup crumbled feta. Mix with your hands just until combined—stop as soon as the feta is evenly dispersed. Overworking ground poultry makes it dense and rubbery.
3. **Roll the Meatballs**: Wet your hands with cold water. Portion the mixture into 12 equal-sized balls (about 1.5 inches wide, or roughly 40g each). Place them on a clean plate.
4. **Preheat the Air Fryer**: Set your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** and preheat for **3 minutes**. A hot basket prevents sticking.
5. **Load and Cook**: Lightly mist the preheated basket with olive oil spray. Place the meatballs inside in a single layer with at least 1/2 inch of space between each. Lightly mist the tops with oil spray. Slide the basket in and cook at **400°F (200°C)** for **10 to 12 minutes**.
6. **The Mid-Point Shake**: At the **6-minute mark**, pull the basket out and give it a firm shake or flip the meatballs using silicone-tipped tongs. Look for pale-golden bottoms that release easily from the basket grate.
7. **Check Doneness**: The meatballs are done when the outside is blistered with deep golden spots and charred feta edges. Insert an instant-read thermometer into the center of the largest meatball; it must read at least **165°F (74°C)**. Pull them immediately to keep them juicy.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **The Meatballs Tear When Flipping**: This happens if you move them too early or didn't oil the basket. If they resist at the 6-minute mark, close the basket and wait another **60 to 90 seconds**; the Maillard reaction will naturally release the meat from the metal once a crust forms.
- **Dry, Tough Texture**: You either used 99% lean white breast meat or overmixed the meat into a paste. Stick to 92/8 ground chicken and fold the ingredients together using your fingers like a claw just until combined.
- **Pale, Soggy Meatballs**: The basket was crowded, trapping steam. If your air fryer basket is under 4 quarts, cook 6 meatballs at a time instead of cramming all 12 into one layer.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Panko Swap**: Don't buy a new container of panko if you're out. Pulse stale sandwich bread into coarse crumbs or crush 8 standard saltine crackers.
- **Feta Swap**: If block feta is too expensive at your store, use grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano. Cut the added kosher salt in half if using Pecorino, as it is naturally saltier.
- **Fresh Parsley Swap**: Skip it completely or swap in 1 teaspoon of dried Italian seasoning or dried mint. Dried mint pairs exceptionally well with lemon and chicken.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge**: Let leftover meatballs cool completely, then store in an airtight container for up to **4 days**.
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Do not microwave these; it turns the chicken rubbery and ruins the crisp outer crust. Place cold meatballs in the air fryer basket, spray lightly with oil, and heat at **350°F (175°C)** for **3 to 4 minutes** until warmed through and crisp on the edges.
