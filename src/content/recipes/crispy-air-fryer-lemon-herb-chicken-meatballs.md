# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Ground chicken is notorious for turning into dry, rubbery hockey pucks in a standard oven. The air fryer fixes this by blasting the exterior with intense convection heat, crisping the outer crust in **12 minutes** flat while locking the steam and fat inside. The secret is keeping excess liquid out of the meat: we rely heavily on fresh lemon zest for punchy citrus flavor and save the lemon juice for serving, preventing the raw mixture from turning into a soggy, unworkable paste.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Appliance Check**: A standard 4-quart (or larger) air fryer basket works best. If you have a smaller 2-to-3-quart model, cook these in two batches. Overcrowding drops the chamber temperature and steams the meatballs instead of frying them.
- **Wet Your Hands**: Ground chicken is wet, loose, and sticky. Keep a small bowl of cold water next to your prep board and dip your palms between rolling each ball. The meat won't stick to your skin.
- **Do Not Skip the Basket Oil**: Chicken meatballs are leaner than beef or pork. Spray or brush the wire basket with avocado or vegetable oil right before loading to prevent tearing the crust when flipping.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Ground Chicken**: 1 lb (450g) – Use 92/8 or 93/7 lean ground chicken. Avoid 99% lean ground chicken breast; it lacks the fat needed to stay juicy.
- **Panko Breadcrumbs**: 1/2 cup (30g) – Standard panko gives a crunchier crust than fine Italian breadcrumbs.
- **Egg**: 1 large – Lightly beaten to bind the mixture without making it dense.
- **Lemon Zest**: 1 tbsp (from 1 medium lemon) – Grate only the yellow skin. Stop when you hit the white pith or it will taste bitter.
- **Garlic**: 3 cloves – Minced fine. Don't use garlic powder here; fresh minced garlic creates better browned bits on the crust.
- **Dried Oregano**: 1 tbsp – Crushed between your palms before adding to release the oils.
- **Fresh Parsley**: 1/4 cup (15g) – Finely chopped, leaves and tender stems.
- **Kosher Salt**: 3/4 tsp – If using table salt, reduce to 1/2 tsp so you don't over-salt.
- **Black Pepper**: 1/2 tsp – Coarsely ground.
- **Olive Oil**: 1 tbsp – Mixed directly into the lean meat to replace missing fat.
- **Cooking Spray**: High-heat oil (avocado or canola) for the basket.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Preheat and Mix**: Set the air fryer to **380°F (193°C)** and let it run empty for **3 minutes**. In a large bowl, combine the ground chicken, panko, beaten egg, lemon zest, minced garlic, oregano, parsley, kosher salt, black pepper, and olive oil. Mix with a fork or clean hands just until combined. Stop mixing as soon as it comes together; overworking ground chicken makes it dense and tough.

2. **Portion and Roll**: Wet your hands with cold water. Scoop out rounded 2-tablespoon portions (about 1.25 oz / 35g each) and roll them lightly into 12 to 14 uniform balls. Place them on a plate; do not stack them.

3. **Load the Basket**: Lightly coat the air fryer grate with cooking oil. Place the meatballs in the basket in a single layer, leaving at least 1/2-inch of space between each one for airflow.

4. **Air Fry**: Cook at **380°F (193°C)** for **12 minutes** total.

5. **Mid-Point Check (at 7 minutes)**: Pull the basket out. The tops should be set and starting to turn opaque and pale golden. Give the basket a firm shake, or use silicone-tipped tongs to nudge and roll each meatball over. This ensures the bottom crisps up evenly.

6. **Check Doneness**: At the **12-minute** mark, check the meatballs. They should be deep golden brown with sizzling crisp edges. Insert an instant-read meat thermometer into the center of the largest ball; it must read at least **165°F (74°C)**. Pull them immediately so they don't dry out from carryover heat.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **The Meatballs Are Tough and Dry**: You bought 99% lean chicken breast, or you overcooked them past 165°F. Fix it by sticking to 92/8 ground chicken and checking internal temp starting at **10 minutes**.
- **The Meatballs Flattened Out and Lost Shape**: The mixture was too warm or wet. If your kitchen is hot, chill the rolled meatballs in the fridge for 15 minutes before air frying so the fat firms up.
- **The Crust Tore Off on the Grate**: You pulled them too early or skipped the oil. Meat naturally releases from the basket once a crust forms. If it sticks at the 7-minute shake, leave it for another 60 seconds before trying to turn them.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Ground Turkey for Chicken**: Use 85/15 or 93/7 ground turkey. It behaves identically in the air fryer and costs roughly the same or less depending on weekly grocery sales.
- **Crushed Crackers for Panko**: Don't buy a brand-new tub of panko if you have saltines or Ritz crackers in the cupboard. Crush them fine; they provide the exact same binding structure and fat-absorbing qualities.
- **Dried Herbs for Fresh**: If fresh parsley is limp or expensive, swap it for 1 teaspoon of dried parsley or dried dill. Don't skip the dried oregano, as that provides the primary Greek flavor profile.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge**: Store cooled meatballs in a shallow, airtight glass or plastic container for up to **4 days**. 
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Never microwave these—the steam will turn the crispy crust into rubber. Toss cold meatballs back into the air fryer basket at **350°F (175°C)** for **3 to 4 minutes**. They will heat through to the center and the exterior will snap back to its original crisp texture.
