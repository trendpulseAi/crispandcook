# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Standard pan-frying chicken meatballs leaves you standing over splattering oil while half the meat sticks to the skillet. Baking them on a sheet tray gives you pale, sad, soggy bottoms. The rapid air circulation in an air fryer sears the outer crust in minutes while locking the juices inside, mimicking a deep fryer without the grease bath. The core trick here is using ground chicken thigh or a 92/8 blend—if you buy bone-dry 99% lean chicken breast, your meatballs will turn into rubber hockey pucks no matter what you do.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Basket Size:** A standard 4-to-6-quart basket holds about 12 to 14 meatballs without crowding. If you have a compact 2-quart basket, cook in two separate batches; crowding causes steaming, not crisping.
- **Preheat the Fryer:** Don't skip this. Getting the basket hot before adding the meatballs sets the bottom crust immediately and prevents sticking.
- **Oil Your Hands:** Ground chicken is notoriously wet and sticky. Lightly coat your palms with olive oil or neutral cooking oil before rolling so the meat releases cleanly into uniform balls.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Ground Chicken (92% lean / 8% fat):** 1 lb (454g) – Don't use 99% fat-free ground chicken breast unless you want dry meatballs.
- **Panko Breadcrumbs:** 1/2 cup (25g) – Panko gives a lighter, crispier bite than dense Italian breadcrumbs.
- **Egg:** 1 large – Acts as your binder; beat lightly before adding.
- **Fresh Lemon Zest:** 1 tbsp (from 1 medium lemon) – Gives sharp lemon punch without adding watery acid that ruins meat texture.
- **Garlic:** 3 cloves, minced fine (about 1 tbsp) – Fresh garlic hits harder than powder here.
- **Dried Oregano:** 1 tsp – The backbone flavor for proper air fryer Greek lemon chicken meatballs.
- **Fresh Parsley:** 2 tbsp, finely chopped – Stems removed. Don't waste money buying fresh dill just for this if you already have parsley.
- **Kosher Salt:** 3/4 tsp – Use 1/2 tsp if using standard fine table salt.
- **Black Pepper:** 1/2 tsp, freshly ground.
- **Olive Oil Spray:** 1-2 seconds of spray – For coating the basket and misting the tops.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Mix the Base:** In a large bowl, whisk the egg, lemon zest, minced garlic, oregano, parsley, salt, and black pepper together first. Add the ground chicken and panko. Mix using clean hands or a fork just until combined. Stop as soon as it comes together—overworking chicken turns it dense.
2. **Portion the Meatballs:** Oil your hands. Scoop roughly 2 tablespoons of meat per ball (about 1.25 oz / 35g each) and gently roll them into 12 to 14 even balls. Place them on a clean plate or cutting board.
3. **Preheat and Load:** Preheat your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** for **3 minutes**. Pull the basket out, give it a quick spray of olive oil, and arrange the meatballs in a single layer with at least 1/2 inch of space between each one. 
4. **Air Fry:** Slide the basket in and cook at **400°F (200°C)** for **10 to 12 minutes**.
5. **The Mid-Point Shake:** At the **6-minute mark**, open the basket and firmly shake it or use silicone tongs to roll each meatball over. They should already be set and releasing easily from the grate. Lightly spray the tops with a touch more oil if they look dry.
6. **Finish Cue:** Check the exterior at **10 minutes**. The edges should be deep golden brown with light char spots. Stick an instant-read meat thermometer into the center of the thickest meatball; pull them out as soon as it reads **165°F (74°C)**.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **The meatballs stick to the basket:** This happens when you put cold meat into a cold basket without oil. Fix: Always preheat the basket for 3 minutes and mist it lightly with high-smoke-point oil right before dropping the meatballs in. Don't try to move them before the 6-minute mark.
- **The texture is dry and chalky:** You bought 99% extra-lean ground chicken breast, or you left them in the basket past 165°F. Fix: Buy 92/8 ground chicken. If you are stuck with 99% lean white meat, mix 1 tablespoon of olive oil directly into the raw meat mixture to reintroduce fat.
- **They flatten into lopsided discs:** The mixture was too wet or sat at room temperature too long. Fix: Pop the rolled meatballs into the freezer for 5 to 7 minutes before cooking to firm up the fat, or add 2 extra tablespoons of panko if the mix feels soupy.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Ground Turkey for Ground Chicken:** 85/15 or 93/7 ground turkey swaps in at a 1:1 ratio with no cook time adjustments. It's often $1 to $2 cheaper per pound.
- **Crushed Crackers for Panko:** If you don't have panko, crush 8 to 10 standard saltines or Ritz crackers in a sandwich bag. They work as a binder and add good seasoning.
- **Dried Herbs for Fresh:** Fresh parsley looks nice, but 1 teaspoon of dried parsley or Italian seasoning from the back of your cupboard works just as well on a weeknight.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge:** Let them cool completely, then pack them into a shallow airtight container. They keep well for **up to 4 days**.
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer:** Do not microwave these; it turns the chicken rubbery and ruins the crisp outer crust. Set your air fryer to **350°F (175°C)** and run the leftover meatballs for **3 to 4 minutes** until hot in the center and crisp on the outside.
