# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Ground chicken dries out fast in a conventional oven, turning into rubbery golf balls long before you get any decent browning. The air fryer fixes that by circulating rapid convection heat at **400°F (200°C)**, frying the outside crisp in **12 minutes** while locking in the juices. The core trick here is using lemon zest instead of lemon juice in the meat mixture; liquid acid breaks down poultry proteins into mush, but zest gives you sharp Greek flavor without compromising the texture. Cleanup takes less than three minutes since all the fat renders straight through the basket grate.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Appliance specifics**: A standard 4-to-6-quart basket air fryer handles 12 to 14 meatballs without crowding. If you use a toaster-oven-style air fryer, use the middle rack position—the top rack will scorch the herb crust before the centers cook through.
- **Wet hands are mandatory**: Ground chicken is wet, sticky, and sloppy compared to beef. Keep a small bowl of cold water next to your prep board and dip your fingers before shaping each ball so the meat doesn't cling to your skin.
- **Mix the binders first**: Whisk your egg, breadcrumbs, zest, and seasonings together *before* adding the raw meat. If you overwork ground chicken while trying to distribute herbs, you develop tough myosin proteins and end up with dense, chewy meatballs.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- Ground chicken (92–93% lean): 1 lb / 450 g – Skip 99% lean ground breast; it has zero fat and will taste like cardboard.
- Panko breadcrumbs: 1/2 cup / 30 g – Japanese-style panko creates pockets of steam for a lighter texture than dense regular breadcrumbs.
- Large egg: 1 – Beaten; holds the shape without making the mix watery.
- Lemon zest: 1 tbsp (from 1 medium lemon) – Freshly grated; avoid the bitter white pith.
- Garlic: 3 cloves – Grated or minced into a paste so you don't bite into raw chunks.
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp – Standard grocery store Greek oregano works best.
- Fresh parsley: 2 tbsp / 8 g – Finely chopped, tough stems discarded.
- Feta cheese: 1/3 cup / 50 g – Crumbled small; creates melted, salty pockets inside.
- Fine sea salt: 3/4 tsp – Reduce to 1/2 tsp if your block of feta is heavily brined.
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp – Coarsely ground.
- Olive oil spray: For the basket and the meatball tops (avoid aerosol cans with propellant that ruin non-stick coatings).

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prep the Binder Base**: In a medium mixing bowl, whisk the beaten egg, 1/2 cup (30 g) panko, 1 tbsp lemon zest, minced garlic, 1 tsp dried oregano, 2 tbsp chopped parsley, 3/4 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp black pepper. Let it stand for **2 minutes** so the breadcrumbs hydrate and soften.
2. **Fold and Shape**: Add the 1 lb (450 g) ground chicken and 1/3 cup (50 g) crumbled feta to the bowl. Use clean hands or a fork to gently fold the mixture together until just combined. Dip your hands in water and roll into 12 equal balls (about 1.5 inches / 4 cm wide, roughly the size of a golf ball).
3. **Preheat and Load**: Preheat your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** for **3 minutes**. Mist the basket lightly with olive oil spray. Place the meatballs inside in a single layer, leaving at least 1/2 inch (1.2 cm) of breathing room around each one. Mist the tops with a quick spray of olive oil to kickstart the browning.
4. **Air Fry**: Cook at **400°F (200°C)** for **10 to 12 minutes** total.
5. **Mid-Point Check**: At the **6-minute mark**, slide the basket out. Give it a firm, controlled shake or use silicone-tipped tongs to roll each meatball over so the bottoms brown evenly. 
6. **Finish Cue**: Pull the meatballs when the exteriors show deep golden edges with charred flecks of feta, and an instant-read meat thermometer inserted into the center reads **165°F (74°C)**. Rest in the warm basket for **2 minutes** before serving to let the juices settle.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **Mistake 1: Meatballs flattening into discs.** Your mixture is too warm or too wet. If you accidentally dumped lemon juice into the mix, stir in an extra 2 tablespoons of panko and chill the rolled balls in the freezer for **10 minutes** before air frying so they hold their structure.
- **Mistake 2: Pale, soggy bottoms.** You crowded the basket. Trapped steam prevents the panko from crisping. If your air fryer is under 4 quarts, cook these in two separate batches of 6 instead of cramming all 12 into one run.
- **Mistake 3: Dry, chalky meat.** You left them in too long or bought 99% lean white meat. Watch the heat here so you don't burn them past **165°F (74°C)**. Pull them the second they hit temp; carryover heat handles the rest.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Ground Turkey for Ground Chicken**: Standard 93/7 ground turkey costs significantly less per pound and handles the exact same cook time without altering the flavor profile.
- **Crushed Crackers for Panko**: Don't buy a brand-new tub of panko if you have saltines or Ritz in the cupboard. Crush them fine; just cut the kosher salt in the recipe by half to compensate for the salted crackers.
- **Dried Herbs for Fresh Parsley**: Don't waste money buying a whole bunch of fresh parsley just to use two tablespoons. Use an extra 1/2 teaspoon of dried oregano or dried basil from your spice rack instead.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge**: Let leftovers cool completely to prevent condensation, then store in an airtight glass container for up to **4 days**.
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Never microwave these—it turns the chicken rubbery and ruins the crisp outer crust. Drop the cold meatballs into the air fryer basket at **350°F (175°C)** for **3 to 4 minutes**. The exterior will crisp back up while the feta warms through to the center.
