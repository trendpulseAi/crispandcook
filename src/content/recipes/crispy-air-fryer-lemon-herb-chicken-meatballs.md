# Crispy Air Fryer Lemon-Herb Chicken Meatballs

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Lean ground chicken dries out into sawdust in a conventional oven long before it develops any real color. The air fryer solves this by blasting the surface with concentrated, circulating heat, creating a crisp golden shell in just **12 minutes** while trapping moisture inside. The trick here is using fresh lemon zest instead of heavy juice for the main flavor—zest packs aromatic citrus oils without adding excess liquid that turns the meat mixture into a soggy paste. A small handful of crumbled feta provides fat pockets that baste the lean poultry from the inside out.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Basket Clearance**: You need a standard 4-quart or larger air fryer to cook a full batch (12–14 meatballs) in one go. If you have a compact 2-quart model, cook in two batches. Crowding makes them steam instead of crisp.
- **Wet Your Hands**: Ground chicken is sticky, wet, and frustrating to roll. Keep a small bowl of cold water nearby and dip your palms before shaping each ball to keep the meat from gluing to your skin.
- **Zest Before You Cut**: Grate the lemon peel while the lemon is whole and firm. Once you slice it open to squeeze the juice, getting good zest off the floppy rind is nearly impossible.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- Ground chicken: 450g / 1 lb (use 92/8 or 85/15 lean-to-fat ratio; avoid 99% fat-free breast meat)
- Panko breadcrumbs: 40g / ½ cup (absorbs steam and creates a lighter texture than fine breadcrumbs)
- Large egg: 1, lightly beaten
- Crumbled feta cheese: 60g / ½ cup (adds necessary fat and salt)
- Lemon zest: 1 tbsp (from 1 medium lemon)
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp / 15 ml
- Garlic: 3 cloves, finely minced
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp / 2g
- Fresh flat-leaf parsley: 2 tbsp / 8g, finely chopped
- Kosher salt: 1 tsp / 5g
- Black pepper: ½ tsp / 1.5g
- Olive oil spray: for the basket and tops of the meatballs

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Hydrate the Panade**: In a large mixing bowl, whisk the beaten egg, lemon zest, lemon juice, minced garlic, dried oregano, kosher salt, black pepper, and breadcrumbs. Let this sit for **2 minutes** until the crumbs absorb the liquid into a soft paste.
2. **Combine Gently**: Add the ground chicken, crumbled feta, and fresh parsley to the bowl. Use clean hands or a fork to mix just until the ingredients are distributed. **Stop mixing immediately once combined**—working the meat too much creates dense, rubbery meatballs.
3. **Portion and Shape**: Lightly wet your hands with cold water. Scoop and roll the mixture into 12 to 14 golf-ball-sized portions (roughly 35g / 1.5 oz each).
4. **Preheat the Air Fryer**: Set the appliance to **400°F (200°C)** and run it empty for **3 minutes**. A hot grate prevents the chicken from sticking.
5. **Load and Cook**: Lightly mist the hot basket with olive oil spray. Arrange the meatballs in a single layer with at least ½ inch (1.2 cm) of space between each. Spray the tops lightly with olive oil to speed up browning. Cook at **400°F (200°C)** for **10 to 12 minutes**.
6. **Mid-Point Shake**: At the **6-minute mark**, slide out the basket and give it a firm shake or use silicone-tipped tongs to roll each ball over. This ensures all sides hit the hot air stream evenly.
7. **Finish Cue**: Check the meatballs at **10 minutes**. They are done when the edges are deep golden brown, slightly blistered, and an instant-read thermometer inserted into the center reads **165°F (74°C)**. Rest in the pan or on a plate for **2 minutes** before serving so the juices settle.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **The Meatballs Stick and Tear**: Chicken has very little surface fat, so it grabs onto metal grates instantly. Don't skip preheating the basket, and always mist the wire bottom with oil spray right before dropping the meatballs in.
- **Tough, Rubbery Meatballs**: This happens when you overwork the raw ground meat or buy 99% fat-free ground chicken breast. Mix only until the feta is evenly dispersed, and stick to standard ground chicken (which includes dark meat).
- **Pale, Wet Surface**: If the meatballs look grey and soft after 8 minutes, your air fryer basket is overcrowded or wasn't preheated. Shake off any excess surface moisture, spread the meatballs out, and run the heat at **400°F (200°C)**.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Ground Turkey for Chicken**: Don't pay extra if ground chicken isn't on sale. Standard 93/7 ground turkey swaps in at a 1:1 ratio with no change to the cooking time.
- **Crushed Crackers for Panko**: If you don't have panko, crush 10 to 12 saltines or Ritz crackers into coarse crumbs. They bind the mixture just as well.
- **Parmesan for Feta**: Don't buy a tub of feta just for one recipe. An equal amount of grated parmesan or sharp white cheddar from your fridge delivers the same salty fat kick.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge**: Store cooked meatballs in a sealed glass or plastic container for up to **4 days**. 
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Skip the microwave—it turns the crust soggy and makes the chicken rubbery. Toss the cold meatballs into the air fryer basket at **350°F (175°C)** for **3 to 4 minutes**. They will heat through to the center while crisping the outside back to day-one texture.
