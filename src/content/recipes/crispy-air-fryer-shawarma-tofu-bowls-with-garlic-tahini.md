# Crispy Air Fryer Shawarma Tofu Bowls with Garlic Tahini

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Baking tofu in a conventional oven takes 45 minutes and often leaves you with leathery, dry cubes. The air fryer’s concentrated heating element and high-speed convection strip surface moisture in minutes, giving you deeply blistered edges while keeping the center tender. Tossing pressed tofu in a little oil *before* dusting with cornstarch and shawarma spices creates a protective crust that traps steam inside. You get restaurant-grade crunch in **15 minutes flat** without deep-frying.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Basket Size**: Use a 4-quart or larger air fryer. If your basket is smaller, cook the tofu in two separate batches; overcrowding traps steam and turns the crust into mush.
- **Tear, Don't Cube**: Cut the block in half, then rip it into bite-sized chunks with your hands. The jagged, rough edges create significantly more surface area for spices to cling to and crisp up better than clean knife cuts.
- **Drying the Curd**: Do not skip pressing. Wrap the block in clean kitchen towels and set a heavy cast-iron skillet on top for at least 10 minutes. Excess water is the number one enemy of crispy air-fried tofu.

## Ingredients & Measurements

### The Shawarma Tofu
- **Extra-firm or super-firm tofu**: 1 block (14 oz / 400g), drained and pressed
- **Olive oil or neutral vegetable oil**: 1.5 tbsp (22ml)
- **Cornstarch**: 1 tbsp (8g) – non-negotiable for blistered edges
- **Ground cumin**: 1 tsp
- **Smoked paprika**: 1 tsp
- **Garlic powder**: 1/2 tsp
- **Ground coriander**: 1/2 tsp
- **Ground turmeric**: 1/4 tsp
- **Ground cinnamon**: 1/4 tsp
- **Kosher salt**: 3/4 tsp
- **Black pepper**: 1/4 tsp
- **Cayenne pepper**: 1/8 tsp (optional, for heat)

### Garlic Tahini Sauce
- **Tahini**: 1/4 cup (60ml) – stir the jar well before measuring
- **Fresh lemon juice**: 2 tbsp (30ml)
- **Garlic**: 1 medium clove, finely grated or minced
- **Warm water**: 3 to 4 tbsp (45 to 60ml) to thin
- **Kosher salt**: 1/4 tsp

### Bowl Assembly
- **Cooked grain base**: 3 cups (approx. 500g) cooked white rice, quinoa, or bulgur
- **English cucumber**: 1 cup (130g), diced
- **Tomatoes**: 1 cup (150g), diced
- **Fresh parsley or pickled red onions**: Optional garnish

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prep and Tear**: After pressing the tofu for 10 minutes, tear it into bite-sized, 3/4-inch jagged chunks. Pat the torn pieces dry once more with a paper towel.
2. **Season**: Place the tofu in a mixing bowl. Drizzle with **1.5 tbsp oil** and toss until every piece is slick. Whisk the cornstarch, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, coriander, turmeric, cinnamon, salt, black pepper, and cayenne in a small dish. Sprinkle the spice mix over the tofu and toss thoroughly until no dry, powdery patches remain.
3. **Load the Air Fryer**: Preheat your air fryer to **390°F (198°C)** for 2 minutes. Add the seasoned tofu to the basket in an even layer. A tiny bit of touching is fine, but do not stack pieces on top of one another.
4. **Air Fry**: Cook at **390°F (198°C)** for **10 minutes** undisturbed. 
5. **Shake**: At the 10-minute mark, pull the basket out and give it an aggressive shake to redistribute the pieces. If any cubes are stuck together, separate them with silicone tongs. Slide the basket back in and cook for an additional **4 to 5 minutes**.
6. **Check Finish Cue**: The tofu is done when the exterior feels rigid and dry to the touch, with visible dark golden browning on the corners. When you shake the basket, the pieces should rattle like dry pebbles.
7. **Whisk the Sauce & Assemble**: While the tofu finishes, whisk the tahini, lemon juice, grated garlic, and salt in a bowl. It will seize up and turn paste-like—that is normal. Whisk in warm water one tablespoon at a time until you get a smooth, pourable sauce. Divide grains, cucumbers, and tomatoes between four bowls, pile on the hot air fryer shawarma tofu, and drizzle generously with the tahini.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)

- **The Tofu Is Soft and Chewy, Not Crunchy**: You skipped pressing the tofu or overloaded the basket. The moisture couldn't escape, so the tofu steamed. Crank the heat to **400°F (204°C)** and run it for another **3 to 4 minutes** while shaking frequently.
- **The Spices Taste Bitter or Burnt**: Ground paprika and garlic powder scorch easily above 400°F. Watch the heat here so you don’t burn it. If your air fryer runs hot, drop the temperature to **375°F (190°C)** next time and add 2 minutes to the cook time.
- **The Tahini Sauce Clumped Into Solid Paste**: Tahini naturally stiffens when mixed with cold acidic liquids. Don't panic and throw it away. Whisk vigorously while adding warm water by the teaspoon; it will break through the curdled stage and emulsify into a silky sauce within 30 seconds.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions

- **Tahini**: Don't waste money buying an expensive jar of sesame paste just for this sauce. Swap it for plain whole-milk Greek yogurt, or dilute standard store-bought hummus with warm water and lemon juice.
- **Cornstarch**: Arrowroot powder, potato starch, or tapioca flour work at a 1:1 ratio. If you have none of these, skip it entirely—the oil and spices will still roast well, just with slightly less shell crunch.
- **Grain Bases**: Skip packaged microwave grain pouches. Use day-old leftover white rice, or dump dried couscous into a bowl with boiling water for 5 minutes. You can also skip grains entirely and use shredded romaine or iceberg lettuce.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions

- **Fridge**: Store leftover tofu in an airtight container for up to **4 days**. Keep the chopped fresh vegetables and tahini sauce in separate containers; mixing them in advance makes the tofu soggy.
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Never microwave leftover tofu—it turns into rubber. Dump cold tofu straight into the air fryer basket at **375°F (190°C)** for **3 to 4 minutes**. It will blister and regain its day-one crunch completely.
