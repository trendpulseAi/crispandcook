# Air Fryer Greek Lemon Herb Beef Meatballs with Blistered Tomatoes

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Convection heat in an air fryer sears the exterior of beef meatballs within minutes, locking in juice before the fat renders out and leaves you with dry hockey pucks. Rolling whole cherry tomatoes into the basket alongside the meat is the real trick here: the tomatoes blister in the rendered beef drippings and herb oil, creating a built-in pan sauce with zero extra pans to wash. You get deeply browned crusts, tender centers, and sweet, burst tomatoes in exactly **12 minutes**.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Appliance size**: A standard 4-quart to 6-quart basket air fryer works best. If your basket is smaller than 4 quarts, cook the meatballs in two batches so they sear instead of steam.
- **Grate the onion, don't chop it**: Diced onion stays crunchy and creates structural weak spots that make meatballs split open. Use the coarse side of a box grater so the onion breaks down into a pulp that blends directly into the meat.
- **Oil your hands before rolling**: Ground beef sticks to warm palms, which tears the meat and ruins the smooth exterior. Lightly rub a few drops of neutral oil or olive oil on your hands before shaping.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Ground beef (85/15 or 80/20)**: 450g / 1 lb – Skip lean 90/10 beef here; it dries out under high air fryer heat.
- **Panko or plain breadcrumbs**: 35g / 1/3 cup – Panko yields a lighter texture, but standard breadcrumbs work fine.
- **Egg**: 1 large – Binds the mixture without making it spongy.
- **Garlic**: 3 cloves, finely minced or grated.
- **Yellow onion**: 1/4 small onion, finely grated (about 2 tablespoons pulp with juices).
- **Lemon zest**: 1 tablespoon (zest of 1 medium lemon; save the juice for serving).
- **Dried oregano**: 1 tablespoon – Rub it between your fingers as you add it to release the oils.
- **Fresh parsley**: 2 tablespoons, finely chopped (optional; omit if you do not want to buy an entire bunch).
- **Crumbled feta cheese**: 60g / 1/2 cup – Buy a solid block in brine and crumble it yourself for better moisture.
- **Kosher salt**: 1 teaspoon (use 3/4 tsp if your feta is exceptionally salty).
- **Black pepper**: 1/2 teaspoon freshly cracked.
- **Cherry or grape tomatoes**: 250g / 1 dry pint, kept whole.
- **Olive oil**: 1 tablespoon – To coat the tomatoes and basket.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Build the meat mixture**: In a large bowl, whisk the egg, grated onion (with its juice), minced garlic, lemon zest, dried oregano, salt, black pepper, and breadcrumbs. Let this sit for **2 minutes** so the breadcrumbs absorb the moisture. Add the ground beef and crumbled feta. Use your fingertips to gently combine until just distributed. Do not pack or overwork the meat, or the meatballs will turn rubbery.
2. **Shape into balls**: Divide into 12 equal-sized portions (about 40g or 1.5 inches each). Roll lightly between oiled hands until smooth. Toss the whole cherry tomatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and a pinch of salt in a small bowl.
3. **Preheat the air fryer**: Set the machine to **400°F (200°C)** for **3 minutes**. A hot basket prevents the meat from sticking to the grates.
4. **Load the basket**: Place the meatballs in the basket spaced at least 1/2 inch apart. Scatter the oiled cherry tomatoes into the empty spaces between the meatballs. 
5. **Cook and blister**: Air fry at **400°F (200°C)** for **8 minutes** undisturbed. 
6. **Mid-point check**: At the **8-minute** mark, pull the basket. The tops should be browned and the tomatoes should look taut and wrinkled. Gently shake the basket or use silicone tongs to roll the meatballs onto their other side and shift the tomatoes so they blister evenly.
7. **Finish cue**: Cook for an additional **3 to 4 minutes** at **400°F (200°C)**. The meatballs are done when an instant-read thermometer hits **160°F (71°C)** in the center and the tomato skins are charred and bursting. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over everything right in the basket before serving.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)
- **The meatballs turn dry and dense**: This happens when you use extra-lean beef (90/10) or knead the meat like bread dough. Mix with a light hand just until the ingredients hold together, and stick to 80/20 or 85/15 ground beef for proper fat content.
- **The tomatoes turn into watery mush**: If you slice the tomatoes before air frying, they dump water into the basket and boil the meat instead of roasting it. Keep them whole; the skins trap steam and blister without collapsing into soup until the final two minutes.
- **Meatballs stick and tear on the basket**: Your air fryer wasn't hot enough when you loaded it, or the grates lack oil. Always run the **3-minute preheat at 400°F (200°C)** and lightly spray or brush the grate with high-heat oil.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions
- **Ground beef**: Swap 1:1 with ground pork or dark-meat ground turkey (85/15). Avoid ground turkey breast (99% lean), as it turns chalky in the air fryer.
- **Feta cheese**: Don't waste money buying a tub of feta if you don't have it. Swap in an equal amount of grated parmesan, or leave cheese out entirely and bump the kosher salt by 1/4 teaspoon.
- **Breadcrumbs**: Use crushed saltines, crushed Ritz crackers, or a slice of stale sandwich bread blitzed in a blender.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions
- **Fridge**: Store leftover meatballs and blistered tomatoes together in an airtight glass container for up to **4 days**. 
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Do not microwave these unless you want rubbery meat. Place cold meatballs and tomatoes in the air fryer basket and heat at **350°F (175°C)** for **4 to 5 minutes**. The exterior will crisp back up while the inside stays juicy.
