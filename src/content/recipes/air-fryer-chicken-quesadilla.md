substitutions:
- swap: cooked chicken
  with: Use drained, cooked black beans for a meat-free version. Warm the filling thoroughly and use a suitable vegetarian cheese if needed.
- swap: cheddar or Monterey Jack
  with: Use another cheese that melts well, such as mozzarella, or a suitable dairy-free alternative.
- swap: red bell pepper
  with: Try a small amount of finely chopped cooked onion or another vegetable. Drain wet ingredients so the tortilla does not become soggy.
steps:
- title: Prepare the filling
  text: Combine the fully cooked chicken, diced pepper, garlic powder, and paprika in a bowl. Shred or chop the chicken into small pieces so the filling heats evenly.
- title: Assemble the quesadillas
  text: Place each tortilla flat. Sprinkle a little cheese over one half, add half of the chicken mixture, and top with the remaining cheese. Fold the empty half over the filling and press gently. Avoid overfilling.
- title: Brush and arrange
  text: Brush the outside of the folded tortillas lightly with oil. Preheat the air fryer to 350°F (175°C) if your appliance recommends it. Place the quesadillas in a single layer without overlapping; cook in batches if necessary.
- title: Air fry
  text: Cook for approximately 7 to 10 minutes, carefully turning halfway through, until the tortillas are golden and the cheese has melted. If the tortillas lift or move, pause the appliance and secure them in a way recommended by its manufacturer, away from the heating element.
- title: Check the filling and serve
  text: Check that the center reaches 165°F (74°C) with a food thermometer, especially when using refrigerated leftover chicken. Continue cooking if needed. Rest briefly, slice into wedges, and serve with salsa or sour cream if desired.
proTips:
- Use a modest amount of filling so the quesadillas stay together and heat evenly.
- If the tortilla browns before the center reaches 165°F (74°C), lower the temperature and continue cooking, checking frequently.
- Serve with a simple green salad, salsa, or sliced avocado for an easy meal.
- Keep fillings dry and cut them into small, even pieces to help prevent a soggy tortilla.
storage: 'Refrigerate leftovers in a covered, shallow container within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use them within 3 to 4 days.


  Reheat in an air fryer or oven until the center reaches 165°F (74°C). Check the filling with a food thermometer rather than relying on a crisp exterior alone. The time needed varies with the thickness and starting temperature.


  For longer storage, freeze cooled portions in freezer-safe packaging. Thaw in the refrigerator before reheating. Tortilla texture may be softer after freezing, but reheating in the air fryer can help restore some crispness.'
faqs:
- q: Can I use leftover chicken?
  a: Yes. Use fully cooked chicken that has been refrigerated safely, and heat the filling to 165°F (74°C) before serving.
- q: Why is my quesadilla soggy?
  a: Too much filling or wet vegetables can soften the tortilla. Use a thin layer of filling, drain wet ingredients, and avoid overlapping quesadillas in the basket.
- q: Can I make it without chicken?
  a: Yes. Try cooked, drained black beans or cooked vegetables with a melting cheese. Keep the filling modest so it warms evenly.

A chicken quesadilla is a comforting way to turn a few simple ingredients into a satisfying lunch or light dinner. This air fryer version combines cooked chicken, melted cheese, and a little sweet bell pepper inside a golden tortilla.

The most important details are keeping the filling fairly thin and checking that the center is hot enough. Air fryers vary, so use the suggested time as a starting point and check the filling with a thermometer.

Serve the quesadillas with salsa, a spoonful of sour cream, or a fresh salad. The filling is flexible, so you can adjust the vegetables and cheese to suit what you have on hand.
