- amount: 4 thick slices

- swap: unsalted butter
  with: Use salted butter and reduce or omit the added salt. A suitable dairy-free spread is another option.

- swap: fresh parsley
  with: Use a small pinch of dried parsley or leave it out.
steps:
- title: Prepare the garlic butter
  text: Mix the softened butter, minced garlic, parsley, and salt in a small bowl until evenly combined.
- title: Spread the bread
  text: Spread a thin, even layer of garlic butter over one side of each bread slice. A thick layer can make the bread greasy rather than crisp.
- title: Arrange in the basket
  text: Preheat the air fryer to 350°F (175°C) if your appliance recommends it. Place the slices buttered-side up in a single layer. Cook in batches if they do not fit without overlapping.
- title: Toast until golden
  text: Air fry for approximately 4 to 6 minutes, checking from the 3-minute mark. The bread is ready when the edges are golden and the garlic butter is hot. Cooking time varies by bread thickness and appliance.
- title: Serve warm
  text: Carefully transfer the bread to a plate and add a little extra parsley if desired. Serve warm alongside soup, pasta, or a salad.
proTips:
- 'Watch the bread closely: garlic and thin edges can brown quickly.'
- Arrange the slices in a single layer so the hot air can reach the surface.
- For a softer center, use thicker slices and begin checking a little earlier.
- Serve with tomato soup, pasta, or a simple green salad.
storage: 'Garlic bread is at its best just after cooking. Refrigerate leftovers in a covered container within 2 hours, or within 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep refrigerated at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 3 to 4 days.


  To reheat, place the bread in the air fryer for a short time at a moderate temperature, checking frequently to prevent burning. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C).


  For longer storage, freeze cooled portions in freezer-safe packaging. Thaw in the refrigerator before reheating. Fresh garlic mixed with butter should be refrigerated promptly rather than stored at room temperature.'

- q: Why did my garlic bread burn?

- q: Can I make this dairy-free?

Garlic bread is a comforting side dish that needs only a few simple ingredients. This air fryer version combines buttery bread with garlic and parsley, creating a golden exterior that pairs well with soup, pasta, or a fresh salad.

The key is to spread the butter evenly and watch the bread as it cooks. Air fryers and slice thicknesses vary, and the edges can turn from golden to too dark quickly.

Make a small batch for a quick meal, or cook the slices in batches if you are serving more people.
