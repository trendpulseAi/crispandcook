steps:
- title: Check your appliance
  text: Use this recipe only if your rice cooker instructions permit oatmeal or porridge. Follow its minimum and maximum fill guidelines. Oats can foam, so do not fill beyond the manufacturer’s porridge limit.

  text: Wash and core the apple, then cut it into small, even pieces. Set a few pieces aside for serving if you like.

storage: 'Refrigerate leftover oatmeal in a shallow, covered container within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use the leftovers within 3 to 4 days.


  Oatmeal thickens as it cools. To reheat, add a small splash of water and stir while warming until it reaches 165°F (74°C) throughout. The exact time depends on the portion size and reheating method.


  You can also freeze portions in freezer-safe containers. Thaw in the refrigerator before reheating, and add fresh toppings after the oatmeal is hot.'
faqs:
- q: Can I make oatmeal in any rice cooker?
  a: No. Use a cooker whose instructions explicitly allow oatmeal or porridge. Oats can foam and overflow in appliances not designed for them.
- q: Can I use steel-cut oats instead of rolled oats?
  a: Steel-cut oats need different liquid amounts and cooking times. Follow your rice cooker’s instructions for that specific type rather than using this recipe unchanged.
- q: Why is my oatmeal too thick?
  a: Oats continue absorbing liquid as they sit. Stir in a little hot water after cooking until the oatmeal reaches the consistency you like.

A bowl of apple cinnamon oatmeal makes a warm, comforting breakfast with just a few everyday ingredients. The apple softens as the oats cook, while cinnamon adds a gentle spice that works well with the creamy texture.

This method is intended for a rice cooker that specifically supports oatmeal or porridge. Oats can foam as they cook, so check your appliance instructions, leave plenty of room in the pot, and monitor the first batch.

Serve the oatmeal as it is or add a little maple syrup and extra cinnamon. If it thickens while resting, a splash of hot water will help loosen it.
