steps:
  - title: "Rinse and season the rice"
    text: "Rinse the rice in a sieve until the water runs mostly clear, then drain well. Add it to the rice cooker with the broth, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and salt. Stir once to distribute the seasonings."
  - title: "Layer in the chicken and vegetables"
    text: "Stir the salsa verde into the rice and broth. Arrange the chicken pieces in a single layer on top, then scatter the drained black beans and frozen corn around them. Do not stir the chicken down into the rice."
  - title: "Cook until safely done"
    text: "Close the lid and run the regular white-rice Cook cycle; do not use a delay-start setting with raw chicken. When the cooker switches to Warm, check the largest chicken piece with an instant-read thermometer: it must reach 165°F (74°C). The chicken should look opaque throughout and the rice should be tender. If the chicken is below temperature or the rice is still firm, close the lid and run another Cook cycle if your cooker allows; otherwise, finish the chicken in a microwave-safe dish until it reaches 165°F (74°C), and add a splash of hot broth to the rice if needed."
  - title: "Rest and finish"
    text: "Once everything is cooked through, let the closed cooker stand on Warm for 5 minutes. Gently fluff the rice and fold in the cheese until melted. Serve with lime, cilantro, and crushed tortilla chips."

This salsa verde chicken and rice is made for a rice cooker: the grains simmer in savory broth while the chicken steams gently on top. The result is a cozy, one-pot dinner with plenty of flavor and very little hands-on cooking.

Black beans and frozen corn make it filling without adding much prep, and a handful of cheese melts into the hot rice at the end. Finish each bowl with lime and cilantro for freshness, plus crushed tortilla chips for a crunchy contrast.
