# Crispy Miso-Glazed Salmon Bites

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Cutting salmon into 1-inch cubes increases the surface area, giving you maximum caramelization from the miso marinade without drying out the fish. The air fryer circulates intense, dry heat around each bite, blistering the sugars in the glaze into a sticky crust in under **8 minutes**. Because the cooking time is so fast, the center stays tender while the exterior develops the dark, savory char you normally only get under a commercial broiler.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Basket Size**: Use a 4-quart or larger air fryer. If you have a compact basket, cook in two separate batches. Cramming the pieces together causes them to steam instead of crisp.
- **Dry the Protein**: Blot the salmon with paper towels until the flesh is bone-dry before cutting. Surface moisture dilutes the miso glaze and creates steam.
- **Even Cuts**: Cut uniform cubes (roughly 1 to 1.25 inches). Uneven pieces will result in small bites turning dry before the thicker ones finish cooking.

## Ingredients & Measurements
- **Skinless salmon fillets**: 1.25 lbs (565g) – budget frozen fillets work great here; thaw completely and pat dry.
- **White miso paste (Shiro miso)**: 2 tbsp (35g) – mild, slightly sweet, and won't overpower the fish.
- **Soy sauce**: 1 tbsp (15ml) – regular or low-sodium.
- **Mirin**: 1 tbsp (15ml) – Japanese sweet cooking wine; adds sheen and balances the salty paste.
- **Toasted sesame oil**: 1 tsp (5ml) – gives fat barrier and aroma.
- **Honey**: 1 tsp (7g) – accelerates surface browning.
- **Fresh ginger**: 1 tsp (3g), finely grated – cuts through the rich salmon fat.
- **Cooking oil spray**: High-smoke point oil (avocado or canola) to coat the basket grate.
- **Optional garnish**: 1 sliced scallion and 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prep the Fish**: Cut 1.25 lbs (565g) of skinless salmon into uniform 1-inch cubes. 
2. **Whisk the Glaze**: In a medium bowl, combine 2 tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tsp honey, and 1 tsp grated ginger. Whisk until smooth and lump-free.
3. **Coat the Salmon**: Drop the salmon cubes into the glaze. Toss gently with a flexible spatula until all sides are slicked. Let sit at room temperature for 5 minutes while the machine heats.
4. **Preheat the Appliance**: Set your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** and preheat for **3 minutes**. Lightly spray the basket grate with cooking oil.
5. **Load the Basket**: Arrange the salmon bites in a single layer with at least 1/2-inch of space between each cube. Do not pile them up.
6. **First Cook Phase**: Air fry at **400°F (200°C)** for **5 minutes** undisturbed. Do not shake the basket; moving them too early tears the glaze off before it sets.
7. **Flip & Finish**: Use silicone-tipped tongs to quickly turn each piece. Air fry for an additional **2 to 3 minutes**. 
8. **Check Doneness**: Look for deeply caramelized, bubbly edges and an opaque center. The internal temperature should read **125°F to 130°F (52°C to 54°C)** on an instant-read thermometer for medium doneness. Pull them immediately to prevent carryover heat from drying them out.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)

- **The Glaze Burns Before the Fish Cooks**: The sugars in miso and honey scorch quickly. If your air fryer runs hot, dial the temperature back to **380°F (193°C)** and check the bites at the 6-minute mark.
- **Salmon Tears and Sticks to the Grate**: Miso acts like glue on bare metal. Always preheat the basket, lightly oil the wire surface right before adding the fish, and leave the pieces untouched for the first 5 minutes so the crust can release naturally.
- **Chalky, Dry Bites**: You left them in too long. Cubed salmon cooks twice as fast as a standard fillet. Pull the basket the second the edges brown and the fish yields to light finger pressure.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions

- **White Miso**: Don't buy an expensive tub of specialty miso if you won't use it again. Swap with 1.5 tbsp hoisin sauce mixed with 0.5 tbsp soy sauce for a similar sweet-savory profile.
- **Mirin**: Replace with 1 tbsp dry white wine or apple cider vinegar mixed with 1/2 tsp granulated sugar.
- **Salmon**: Steelhead trout has an identical fat profile and texture, but it often sells for $2 to $4 less per pound than Atlantic salmon.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions

- **Fridge**: Store leftover air fryer miso salmon bites in a sealed glass container for up to **3 days**. Glass prevents lingering fish oils from absorbing into plastic.
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer**: Never microwave these—it ruins the texture and makes the salmon smell fishy. Preheat your air fryer to **350°F (175°C)**. Add the cold bites and cook for **3 to 4 minutes** until the exterior is sizzling and warmed through.
