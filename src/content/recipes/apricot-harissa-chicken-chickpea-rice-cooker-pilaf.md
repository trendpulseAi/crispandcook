This pilaf is made for the rice cooker: it gently steams the chicken while the basmati absorbs a warmly spiced broth. Harissa brings a little smoky heat, and chickpeas, carrots, and apricots turn the rice into a satisfying one-pot meal.

There’s no need to brown anything first or keep watch over a saucepan. Just layer the ingredients into the cooker, let its regular rice cycle do the work, and finish with lemon and parsley for a bright, fresh lift.
