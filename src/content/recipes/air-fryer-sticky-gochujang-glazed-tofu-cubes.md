# Air Fryer Sticky Gochujang Glazed Tofu Cubes

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Most glazed tofu recipes tell you to air fry the tofu with the sauce on. That is a mistake; the sugars in fermented chili paste burn to a bitter charcoal long before the tofu crisps. This recipe works because we air fry naked, cornstarch-dusted cubes at **400°F (200°C)** so the circulating dry heat blisters the exterior into a shatteringly crisp shell. Once the cubes are rigid and golden, you toss them in a warm, glossy reduction off the heat, giving you perfect **air fryer crispy gochujang tofu** that stays crunchy instead of turning into a soggy sponge.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Appliance specifics:** Tested in a standard 5.8-quart basket-style air fryer. If you are using a compact 3-quart model, cook the tofu in two separate batches. Crowding the basket traps steam and ruins your crunch.
- **Drying is non-negotiable:** Wrap the block of tofu in clean kitchen towels or paper towels and set a heavy cast-iron skillet on top for at least **8 minutes**. Pressing out the excess brine is the difference between leathery chew and a light, crisp bite.
- **Cube sizing:** Cut cubes to roughly 3/4-inch (2 cm). Anything smaller will dry out completely in the center; anything larger will have too much soft interior relative to the crust.

## Ingredients & Measurements

### The Crispy Tofu
- **Extra-firm tofu:** 1 block (14 oz / 400 g) – *Do not buy firm or soft tofu; only extra-firm holds its shape and crisps properly.*
- **Cornstarch:** 1.5 tbsp (12 g) – *Potato starch works identically.*
- **Neutral cooking oil:** 1 tbsp (15 ml) – *Canola, vegetable, or avocado oil. Avoid unrefined oils with low smoke points.*
- **Fine salt:** 1/4 tsp (1.5 g)

### The Sticky Gochujang Glaze
- **Gochujang (Korean chili paste):** 2 tbsp (32 g) – *Standard tub paste; adjust down to 1 tbsp if you are sensitive to heat.*
- **Low-sodium soy sauce:** 1.5 tbsp (22 ml) – *Use tamari if cooking gluten-free.*
- **Maple syrup or brown sugar:** 1.5 tbsp (22 ml / 18 g) – *Provides the tacky stickiness needed to cling to the crust.*
- **Rice vinegar:** 1 tsp (5 ml) – *Cuts through the dense fermented paste with acidity.*
- **Toasted sesame oil:** 1 tsp (5 ml) – *Adds aroma; do not skip.*
- **Garlic:** 1 clove, finely minced or grated (about 1 tsp)
- **Water:** 1 tbsp (15 ml) – *To thin the glaze so it coats evenly.*
- **Toasted sesame seeds:** 1 tsp (optional garnish)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Dry and coat:** Press the tofu for **8–10 minutes** under a heavy skillet. Cut into 3/4-inch (2 cm) uniform cubes. Drop the cubes into a medium bowl, drizzle with **1 tbsp (15 ml) neutral oil**, and toss gently until the surfaces glisten. Sprinkle **1.5 tbsp (12 g) cornstarch** and **1/4 tsp salt** over the top. Toss until a powdery, even matte layer forms on every cube with no bare wet spots.
2. **Load the air fryer:** Preheat your air fryer to **400°F (200°C)** for **2 minutes**. Dump the coated tofu into the basket and spread the cubes out so none are stacked on top of each other. A tiny gap between each piece allows air circulation.
3. **Air fry:** Cook at **400°F (200°C)** for **14 minutes** total. 
4. **Mid-point shake:** At the **8-minute mark**, pull the basket out and give it an aggressive shake to loosen any sticking pieces and flip the surfaces. The edges should look pale golden and feel lightly firm to the touch. Slide the basket back in for the remaining **6 minutes**.
5. **Simmer the glaze:** While the tofu finishes, add the gochujang, soy sauce, sweetener, rice vinegar, sesame oil, minced garlic, and water to a wide skillet or saucepan. Place over medium-low heat for **2 minutes**, stirring constantly with a rubber spatula until bubbly, smooth, and slightly thickened. Turn the heat completely off.
6. **Finish and toss:** When the timer goes off, verify doneness: tap a cube with a fork—it should sound hollow and feel completely hard and blistered. Immediately dump the hot tofu directly into the skillet with the warm glaze. Toss quickly for **30 seconds** until every side is lacquered and sticky. Garnish with sesame seeds and serve immediately.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)

- **The tofu turns out soft and rubbery:** You skipped the pressing step or crammed all the cubes into a tiny basket. If the moisture has nowhere to escape, the tofu steams in its own water. **The fix:** Press the block thoroughly next time, and if your air fryer is under 4 quarts, cook in two batches.
- **The glaze burns or turns bitter:** You added the sauce into the air fryer basket with the raw tofu. Gochujang contains sugar and starches that scorch at **400°F (200°C)** within 4 minutes. **The fix:** Always crisp the tofu dry first, then toss in the reduced sauce in a separate pan off the heat.
- **Tofu sticks to the wire basket:** The basket wasn't hot or the tofu lacked oil. **The fix:** Give the air fryer basket a light rub with an oiled paper towel (or use an oil mister, never aerosol cooking spray which ruins non-stick coatings) right before adding the cubes, and don't skip tossing the tofu in oil before the cornstarch.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions

- **Maple syrup substitute:** Don't waste money buying real maple syrup for this sauce if you don't have it. Use standard light brown sugar, honey, or even budget pancake syrup at a 1:1 ratio.
- **Rice vinegar substitute:** If your pantry lacks rice vinegar, use equal parts apple cider vinegar or plain white distilled vinegar diluted with a splash of water. It provides the exact same acid profile needed to balance the sweet paste.
- **Cornstarch substitute:** Tapioca starch or potato starch works just as well to pull off that dry exterior shell. Avoid regular all-purpose flour; it turns doughy and pasty rather than glass-crisp in an air fryer.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions

- **Fridge:** Store sauced leftovers in an airtight glass container for up to **3 days**. The glaze will soften the exterior over time, but the flavor will deeply penetrate the tofu.
- **Reheat in the Air Fryer:** Do not microwave this—it will turn the cubes into rubbery sponges. Preheat your air fryer to **360°F (180°C)**. Line the basket with a small piece of perforated parchment paper to prevent sticky glaze from burning onto the basket grates. Air fry for **4 to 5 minutes** until heated through and bubbling. Watch the heat closely so the sugars in the glaze do not char.
