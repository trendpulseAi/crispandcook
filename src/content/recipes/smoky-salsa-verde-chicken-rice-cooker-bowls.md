This smoky salsa verde chicken and rice is made for the Rice Cooker: the grains soak up tangy salsa and savory broth while small pieces of chicken steam gently on top. Black beans and corn turn it into a hearty dinner, all without juggling multiple pots.

It’s a practical, budget-conscious meal for busy evenings, and the ingredients are easy to adapt to what you have on hand. A squeeze of lime and a handful of cilantro add a fresh finish just before serving.
