steps:
- title: Wash and dry
  text: Scrub the potatoes under running water to remove any dirt, then dry them thoroughly with a clean towel. Dry skins are better suited to browning.
- title: Pierce and season
  text: Prick each potato several times with a fork. Rub the skins lightly with oil, then sprinkle them with salt.
- title: Heat the air fryer
  text: Preheat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C) if your appliance instructions recommend it. Place the potatoes in the basket with space between them so the hot air can circulate.
- title: Cook until tender
  text: Air fry for approximately 35 to 50 minutes, turning the potatoes halfway through. Smaller potatoes may be ready sooner, while larger ones may need extra time.
- title: Check and serve
  text: Carefully test the center with a fork or small knife; it should slide in easily. If the center is still firm, cook a little longer and check again. Let the potatoes rest briefly, then split them open carefully and add the toppings you like.
proTips:
- Choose potatoes of similar size so they finish at roughly the same time.
- Dry the skins after washing and leave space around the potatoes in the basket.
- For a simple meal, add cooked beans and cheese, or serve alongside chicken and a salad.
- Use a towel or oven mitt when handling the hot potatoes, and watch for steam when splitting them.
storage: 'Remove any perishable toppings and refrigerate leftover potatoes in a shallow, covered container within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use leftovers within 3 to 4 days.


  To reheat, use the air fryer or oven and heat until the center reaches 165°F (74°C). The time varies with the size of the potato and whether it is whole or split. Add fresh toppings after reheating.


  Freezing can change the texture of baked potatoes, so refrigeration is a good choice when you plan to eat them soon. If you do freeze them, cool and package them promptly, then thaw in the refrigerator before reheating.'
faqs:
- q: How long do baked potatoes take in the air fryer?
  a: Medium potatoes may take around 35 to 50 minutes at 400°F (200°C). Size and appliance performance affect the time, so check that the center is tender.
- q: Do I need to wrap the potatoes in foil?
  a: No. Cooking them unwrapped allows the hot air to reach the skins. Follow your appliance manufacturer’s guidance when using foil.
- q: Why is my baked potato still firm inside?
  a: It may be larger than expected or need more time. Return it to the air fryer and check it again until a fork or small knife slides easily into the center.

A baked potato can be a comforting side dish or the beginning of a simple meal. This air fryer version uses just potatoes, a little oil, and salt, leaving plenty of room for your favorite toppings.

The goal is a nicely browned skin and a soft, fluffy center. Because potatoes vary in size and air fryers cook differently, check them for tenderness rather than relying on the clock alone.

Add butter and chives for a classic serving, or try cooked beans and cheese for a more substantial plate. The recipe is easy to adjust to suit the rest of your meal.
