Tender, cubed salmon gets tossed in oil and starch, then air-fried until the edges crisp up like crackling before getting coated in a sticky, sweet-heat glaze.

The real secret here is timing the glaze. Brush the hot honey gochujang mixture on during the final minute of cooking so the sugars caramelize without scorching into bitter black carbon.
