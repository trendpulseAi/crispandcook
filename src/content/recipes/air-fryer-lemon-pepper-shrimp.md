steps:
- title: Prepare the shrimp
  text: If frozen, thaw the shrimp in the refrigerator before starting; thawing time is not included in the prep time. Pat dry with paper towels and remove any remaining shell pieces. Leave the tails on for presentation or remove them for easier eating.
- title: Toss with seasoning
  text: Mix the oil, lemon zest, black pepper, garlic powder, and salt in a bowl. Add the shrimp and toss to coat evenly. Keep the lemon juice for after cooking.
- title: Arrange in one layer
  text: Preheat the air fryer to 390°F (200°C) if recommended by the manufacturer. Arrange shrimp in a single layer with a little space between them. Cook in batches if needed.
- title: Cook and check early
  text: Air fry for about 5 to 8 minutes, turning halfway. Start checking at 5 minutes. Cook until the centers reach 145°F (63°C) and the flesh is pearly and opaque. Timing varies with shrimp size and machine; do not rely on color alone.
- title: Finish and serve
  text: Transfer to a clean plate, drizzle with lemon juice, and add optional parsley. Serve immediately with rice, salad, or tortillas. Do not return cooked shrimp to a plate that held them raw unless it has been washed.
proTips:
- Use raw rather than precooked shrimp; precooked shrimp can become tough with this timing.
- A tightly curled shrimp can indicate overcooking; temperature is a more reliable check than shape.
- Cook similar sizes together and check the smallest shrimp first.
storage: 'Refrigerate leftover shrimp in a shallow, covered container within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the surrounding temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Keep at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 3 to 4 days. Store rice and other sides separately where practical so the shrimp can be reheated briefly without drying out while the sides warm through. Never leave the serving plate out throughout a long afternoon.


  For reheating, place the shrimp in a small oven-safe dish approved for your air fryer. Add a teaspoon of water and heat at 300°F (150°C), checking after about 2 minutes and continuing only as needed to reach 165°F (74°C) throughout. Shrimp toughen with repeated heating, so warm only the amount you plan to eat. Freeze cooled portions in airtight freezer-safe packaging for up to 2 months for best quality. Thaw in the refrigerator, not on the counter. Add fresh lemon juice and herbs after reheating rather than before freezing.'
faqs:
- q: Can I cook these from frozen?
  a: This recipe is written for fully thawed raw shrimp. Frozen shrimp require different timing and can release extra water, so thaw first for more predictable results.
- q: Why are my shrimp rubbery?
  a: Overcooking is the most common cause. Check early, use similarly sized shrimp, and remove them when they reach the safe temperature.
- q: Can I skip the salt?
  a: Yes. Leave it out or adjust to taste, especially if your shrimp or seasoning already contains added salt.

Lemon, black pepper, and a little garlic give these shrimp plenty of flavor without a long ingredient list. The air fryer handles the cooking in minutes, making this a useful protein to serve with rice or tuck into warm tortillas.

Use fully thawed raw shrimp and dry them before seasoning. A crowded basket traps moisture, so cook in batches if that is what your appliance needs.

Finish with lemon juice after cooking and serve straight away. The shrimp should be tender, not dry or tightly curled.
