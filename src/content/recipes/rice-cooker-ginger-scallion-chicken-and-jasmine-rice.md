# Rice Cooker Ginger Scallion Chicken and Jasmine Rice

## Why This Recipe Works (No BS)
Cooking chicken directly on top of rice uses the sealed steam of your rice cooker to do two jobs at once. As the chicken cooks, its juices and fat render straight down into the grains, flavoring the jasmine rice from the inside out. The core trick is keeping the chicken elevated on the rice bed so the bottom heating element doesn't scorch the meat before the starch cooks through. You get juicy chicken and savory rice in a single cycle with one pot to wash.

## Equipment & Prep Notes
- **Rice Cooker Size**: Any standard 4-cup to 10-cup uncooked capacity rice cooker (standard fuzzy logic or basic one-button models). If using a mini 2-cup cooker, halve the recipe so the pot doesn't boil over.
- **Rinse the Starch**: Rinse your jasmine rice in cold water 3 to 4 times until the water runs mostly clear. Excess surface starch will turn the bottom into glue instead of fluffy grains.
- **Dry the Chicken**: Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels before seasoning. Excess surface moisture throws off the liquid-to-rice ratio inside the sealed pot.

## Ingredients & Measurements

### The Chicken & Rice Base
- **Jasmine rice**: 2 cups (380g) – standard long-grain jasmine; do not use parboiled or brown rice here.
- **Chicken broth or water**: 2 cups (480ml) – low-sodium chicken broth gives better flavor, but plain tap water works if you're pinching pennies.
- **Boneless, skinless chicken thighs**: 1.25 lbs (570g) – roughly 4 to 5 thighs. Skip chicken breasts; they turn to rubber in the steam.
- **Soy sauce**: 1 tbsp (15ml) – standard regular soy sauce (not dark).
- **Sesame oil**: 1 tsp (5ml) – for the rice seasoning.
- **Garlic**: 3 cloves, finely minced.
- **Fresh ginger**: 1 tbsp (15g), finely grated.
- **Kosher salt**: 1 tsp (6g), divided.

### The Ginger Scallion Sauce
- **Scallions (green onions)**: 1 large bunch (about 6 to 7 stalks), thinly sliced, dark greens separated from the white parts.
- **Fresh ginger**: 2 tbsp (30g), very finely minced or grated.
- **Neutral cooking oil**: 3 tbsp (45ml) – canola, vegetable, or peanut oil.
- **Kosher salt**: 1/2 tsp (3g).

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Rinse and Season the Rice**: Place **2 cups (380g)** of jasmine rice into the rice cooker inner pot. Rinse thoroughly with cold water and drain completely. Pour in **2 cups (480ml)** of chicken broth, **1 tbsp (15ml)** soy sauce, **1 tsp (5ml)** sesame oil, **1/2 tsp (3g)** salt, the minced garlic, the grated ginger, and the white parts of the sliced scallions. Stir gently to combine and level the rice flat with a spoon.
2. **Layer the Meat**: Season both sides of the chicken thighs with the remaining **1/2 tsp (3g)** salt. Lay the chicken flat on top of the rice in a single layer. Do not shove the chicken down to the bottom of the pot; it must sit directly on the surface of the rice bed.
3. **Run the Cook Cycle**: Close the lid. Select the standard **White Rice** cycle (or press the single switch down to **Cook** on basic models). Let the machine run its full cycle, which takes roughly **30 to 35 mins**. Do not open the lid while the cycle is running, or you will vent the steam needed to cook the meat through.
4. **Make the Sizzle Sauce**: While the cooker runs, prep the topping. Place the sliced green scallion tops, **2 tbsp (30g)** minced ginger, and **1/2 tsp (3g)** salt into a heatproof bowl. Heat **3 tbsp (45ml)** neutral oil in a small skillet over medium-high heat for **2 mins** until shimmering (it should faint-smoke slightly). Pour the hot oil directly over the scallion mixture. It will sizzle vigorously. Stir and set aside.
5. **Check Doneness and Rest**: When the rice cooker clicks to "Warm", pop the lid and insert an instant-read thermometer into the thickest part of the chicken. It must read at least **165°F (74°C)**. Transfer the chicken to a cutting board, close the rice cooker lid, and let the rice steam on the "Warm" setting for **5 mins**. Slice the chicken, fluff the rice with a paddle, and serve topped with the ginger scallion sauce.

## What Can Go Wrong (And How to Fix It)

- **The rice on the bottom is burnt while the chicken is raw**: You shoved the chicken down into the water, preventing the rice from absorbing the liquid properly, or your cooker runs excessively hot. **Fix**: Next time, float the chicken gently on top. For now, pull the chicken out, microwave it for **2 mins** until it hits **165°F (74°C)**, and leave the scorched bottom layer of rice behind.
- **The rice came out mushy and wet**: You didn't drain the rinse water completely before measuring the broth, adding extra water to the pot. **Fix**: Open the lid, fluff the top layer gently with a fork, and leave the cooker on the "Warm" setting with the lid cracked open for **7 to 10 mins** to let excess steam evaporate.
- **The chicken is dry and stringy**: You used chicken breasts instead of thighs. Breasts cannot handle the high moisture and prolonged heat of a standard rice cook cycle. **Fix**: Dice the dry chicken fine and toss it directly into the ginger scallion oil so the fat coats the meat fibers.

## Cheap Swaps & Pantry Substitutions

- **The Chicken**: If boneless skinless thighs are overpriced, buy bone-in skin-on thighs. Pull the skins off with a paper towel and drop them in bone-and-all. Add **5 mins** to the resting time to ensure the meat next to the bone hits **165°F (74°C)**.
- **The Broth**: Don't waste money buying liquid broth cartons if you don't have them. Use plain water mixed with 1 teaspoon of bouillon powder or a single bouillon cube dissolved in warm water.
- **The Aromatics**: Fresh ginger is mandatory for the finishing sauce, but if you run short for the rice base, substitute **1/2 tsp ground ginger** directly into the cooking liquid.

## Storage & Reheating Instructions

- **Fridge**: Store leftover chicken and rice together in an airtight container for up to **4 days**. Keep the ginger scallion sauce in a separate small jar for up to **1 week**.
- **Reheat in the Rice Cooker**: Place the cold rice and sliced chicken back into the rice cooker pot. Sprinkle **2 tbsp (30ml)** of water over the rice to restore moisture. Close the lid and hit the **Quick Cook** or **Cook** cycle for **8 to 10 mins** until hot, or let it sit on the **Warm** setting for **15 mins** until the chicken reaches **165°F (74°C)**. Spoon fresh, cold ginger scallion sauce over the top right before eating.
