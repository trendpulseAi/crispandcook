steps:

    text: "Preheat the Air Fryer to 380°F (193°C) if your model recommends preheating. Arrange the chicken in a single layer and cook for 8 minutes. If needed, continue cooking in 2-minute increments."

The Air Fryer gives chicken thighs lovely browned edges while keeping the inside tender, and a quick brush of apricot-chile glaze turns them glossy and flavorful. Because the glaze goes on near the end, it gets sticky without scorching.

Cauliflower cooks in the same appliance with just a little oil and cumin, coming out tender with crisp, golden edges. Cook it after the chicken so both have room in the basket, then serve them together for an easy dinner made with affordable, everyday ingredients.
