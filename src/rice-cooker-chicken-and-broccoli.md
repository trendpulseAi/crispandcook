steps:
  - title: "Marinate the chicken"
    text: "In a bowl, mix the chicken with 1 tbsp soy sauce, the garlic powder, and the cornstarch. Let it sit while you rinse the rice."
  - title: "Add rice and broth"
    text: "Put the rinsed rice and chicken broth into the rice cooker pot. Shake the pot gently so the rice lies flat."
  - title: "Layer the chicken on top"
    text: "Spread the chicken evenly over the rice. Don't stir it in. Close the lid and start the regular white rice setting."
  - title: "Add the broccoli"
    text: "When the cooker switches to Keep Warm, quickly open the lid, add the broccoli on top, and close it again. Let it steam on Keep Warm for 10 minutes."
  - title: "Finish and serve"
    text: "Check that the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C). Drizzle with the remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce and the sesame oil, fluff gently, and top with green onions."
proTips:
  - "Use regular US measuring cups for this recipe, not the small cup that came with your rice cooker. They're different sizes."
  - "Don't stir the chicken into the rice before cooking. It blocks the steam and can leave crunchy rice underneath."
  - "Small rice cookers (3 cups or less) may overflow. Halve the recipe if yours is small."
  - "A nonstick inner pot helps a lot here. If rice sticks at the bottom, let it rest on Keep Warm 5 more minutes before scooping."
storage: |
  Let leftovers cool, then move them to an airtight container within 2 hours of cooking. They keep in the fridge for 3 to 4 days. Rice dries out in the fridge, so pack it loosely instead of pressing it down.

  This meal freezes well for up to 2 months. Divide it into single portions in freezer-safe containers or bags, flatten the bags so they freeze and thaw faster, and label them with the date. The broccoli gets a bit softer after freezing, but the taste stays good.

  To reheat, sprinkle 1 to 2 tablespoons of water over each portion, cover, and microwave for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring halfway, until it's 165°F (74°C). The water re-steams the rice so it doesn't turn hard. You can also reheat it in the rice cooker: add a splash of water, set it to Keep Warm, and leave it for 15 to 20 minutes.
faqs:
  - q: "Is it safe to cook raw chicken in a rice cooker?"
    a: "Yes, as long as it reaches 165°F (74°C). Small 1-inch pieces of thigh cook through during a normal white rice cycle. Check one with a thermometer, and if it's not there yet, let it sit on Keep Warm for 5 to 10 minutes more."
  - q: "Why is my rice mushy or crunchy?"
    a: "Mushy usually means too much liquid or skipping the rinse. Crunchy usually means the chicken was stirred into the rice or the lid was opened during cooking. Keep the chicken on top and the lid closed until the cycle ends."
  - q: "Can I use frozen chicken?"
    a: "Thaw it first. Frozen chicken releases water, adds liquid to the rice, and may not reach a safe temperature by the end of the cycle."

If you own a rice cooker and only use it for plain rice, you're missing out. Put the chicken right on top of the rice, press one button, and dinner cooks itself.

This is the kind of meal for nights when you have zero energy. About 10 minutes of chopping, then you walk away. The chicken juices drip into the rice while it cooks, so every bite tastes seasoned.
