  You can freeze cooked salmon bites for up to 2 months, but the texture gets a bit softer. Freeze them in a single layer on a tray first, then move them to a freezer bag so they don't stick together. Thaw overnight in the fridge.

  To reheat, put the bites back in the air fryer at 350°F (175°C) for 3 to 4 minutes. Keep the heat lower than when you cooked them, because high heat dries reheated salmon out fast. Salmon feels fancy, but cut into bites it's one of the fastest dinners you can make. Eight minutes in the air fryer, a quick sticky glaze, done.

The trick is simple: cook the salmon plain, then toss it in the glaze at the end. You get crispy edges, a soft middle, and no burnt honey glued to your basket.
